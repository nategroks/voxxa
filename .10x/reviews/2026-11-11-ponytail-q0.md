## 4. Ponytail audit of the May branch

# Voxxa audit: May branch (`C:/dev/lamport`, commit f7b56e6)

**What this repo does:** Voxxa is a desktop app for churches, built with Tauri 2: a Rust backend with a plain JavaScript screen. It listens to the room through a microphone and turns speech into text with Whisper, a speech-to-text model that runs on the laptop. It guesses which song and which verse is being sung, then moves the slides in the presentation app (ProPresenter, OpenLP, FreeShow, OpenSong, or plain key presses) and blanks the screen when no one is singing.

**Load I assumed:** one church laptop (most likely Windows), one operator, one service at a time, with setlists of 4 to 10 songs. Some inputs are not trusted: imported files (pptx, PDF, EasyWorship database, OpenLyrics), a local HTTP API, and network peers. The worst failure is a wrong slide on the screen during worship. A blank screen is acceptable.

---

## Must fix

### 1. Speech that matches no song puts the old lyrics back on screen
- **What this is:** `Conductor::on_transcript` handles each new piece of text from Whisper (`src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:264-343`).
- **Problem:** When text arrives and the screen is blank, lines 333-340 show the current song's slide again. They never check that the words match that song. I copied the aligner into a scratch program and ran it. After a song, 10 s of silence blanked the screen. Then "let us bow our heads and pray…" returned `Goto{song 0, slide 0}` and `blank=false`, so the lyrics came back during the prayer. Also, nothing ever blanks on speech that matches no song: `unrecognized_speech_to_blank_secs` is only used as a silence timer (line 245). So if the pastor starts talking right after the last song, the lyrics stay up for the whole sermon.
- **Fix:** Unblank only when the words match the current song, for example when that song's raw score is at least `min_song_match`. Start a timer when speech does not match, and blank once it passes `unrecognized_speech_to_blank_secs`.
- **If we skip it:** Song lyrics show during prayers, announcements and the sermon, every week.

### 2. "Unblank" presses the same toggle key as "blank", so the screen goes black at the wrong times
- **What this is:** The keystroke driver (the default) and the OpenSong driver have no separate unblank. Unblank sends the blank toggle again (`presenters/keystroke.rs:183-186`, `presenters/opensong.rs:156-160`).
- **Problem:** The code that sends commands to the presentation app (the dispatcher) does not know if that app's screen is black right now. At start the conductor thinks the screen is blank, but nothing was sent. So when the first song is detected, the dispatcher calls `unblank()` (`commands/mod.rs:454-467`), which presses "." and turns the screen black. After that, every blank and unblank does the opposite of what was meant. For example, the end-of-song blank makes the lyrics visible again.
- **Fix:** In the dispatcher, keep one boolean for "what we last sent to the presenter". Only press the toggle key when the wanted state is different from that.
- **If we skip it:** With the default driver, the screen goes black when singing starts and lights up when singing stops.

### 3. Key presses are typed into Voxxa's own window and loop forever
- **What this is:** The keystroke driver sends real key presses to whichever window has focus (`keystroke.rs:107-116`). Voxxa's own window reacts to those same keys: → next, ← previous, "." blank (`src/main.js:1634-1665`).
- **Problem:** When the operator clicks Start, Next or Blank in Voxxa, the Voxxa window has focus. The synthetic "." or → then goes to Voxxa, which clicks its own Blank or Next button, which sends the key again, and so on without end. Opening the Stage Display also steals focus, because it calls `set_focus` (`commands/mod.rs:922`). After that, every key goes to that window and the presentation app never moves, while Voxxa believes it did.
- **Fix:** Before sending a key, check whether any Voxxa window has focus (`WebviewWindow::is_focused`). If it does, refuse and show "focus your presentation app". Also stop `toggle_stage_display` from calling `set_focus`.
- **If we skip it:** One click in Voxxa can start an endless loop of blank toggles or slide moves, and the slide position is lost.

### 4. Voxxa's slide numbers do not match the presentation app's slide numbers
- **What this is:** `Goto` carries `global_index`, a slide number counted across the whole setlist. Drivers that can jump use it as an index into the presentation that is open right now: `propresenter7_rest.rs:199-201` (`/v1/presentation/active/{index}`), `propresenter7_ws.rs:231-245` (`"0:0"` = the selected presentation), `freeshow.rs:162-164`, `opensong.rs:163-166`.
- **Problem:** If each song is its own presentation (the normal ProPresenter playlist setup), song 3 slide 1 is sent as "slide 12 of the open presentation". That is the wrong slide, or nothing. The importers also drop slides the presentation app still has: blank pptx slides (`importers/pptx.rs:50`), empty PDF pages (`pdf.rs:26`), and repeated .pro7 blocks (`pro7.rs:42`). So with next/previous keys, every slide after a dropped one is shown one step off.
- **Fix:** For jump-capable drivers, send `slide_in_song` and refuse to jump unless the app reports the right presentation (Pro7 REST can do this through `current_state`). In the importers, keep empty slides as placeholders with empty text and skip them only when matching.
- **If we skip it:** Wrong slides on screen with ProPresenter, FreeShow and OpenSong, and an off-by-one with pptx and PDF decks.

### 5. A detected song always starts at slide 1, even halfway through the song
- **What this is:** `start_song` always sets `current_slide_in_song = 0` (`aligner/mod.rs:421-435`). Within a song, the conductor only ever moves one slide forward (`:391-406`).
- **Problem:** Say listening restarts during verse 3: after a mic dropout, after a stop and start, or after the operator edits a slide. The slide editor reloads the setlist, and `load_setlist` resets the conductor and `last_dispatched_global` (`commands/mod.rs:132-133`, `src/main.js:681-697`). When the song is found again, drivers that can jump go back to verse 1 while the room sings verse 3. Voxxa then cannot catch up, because it only compares the current slide with the next one.
- **Fix:** In `start_song`, pick the slide in that song that best matches the recent words. When only text changes, update it in place instead of calling `load_setlist`.
- **If we skip it:** Fixing a typo during the service, or any restart, sends the screen back to verse 1.

### 6. Downloading a model freezes slide control for the whole download
- **What this is:** `download_model` (`commands/mod.rs:642-665`).
- **Problem:** The comment says the lock is dropped before the download, but the code holds the `transcription` lock (the guard that lets only one task use the speech engine at a time) across the full download. The listening loop needs that lock to transcribe (`:319`, `blocking_lock`), and so does `get_status` (`:544`). If someone starts a 1.5 GB download while Voxxa is listening, the loop stops: no slide moves, no blanking, no level meter.
- **Fix:** Copy the URL and the file path while holding the lock, drop the lock, then download.
- **If we skip it:** The screen stays frozen on whatever slide was showing until the download ends.

### 7. Every `listen()` call is rejected, so the screen, the Stage Display and the tray never update
- **What this is:** The app has no `src-tauri/capabilities/` folder, and `gen/schemas/capabilities.json` is `{}`. Tauri 2 allows plugin commands only when a capability (permission file) grants them (`tauri-2.10.3/src/webview/mod.rs:1802-1830`). The event API is a plugin command: `plugin:event|listen`, in Tauri's `bundle.global.js`.
- **Problem:** `setupListeners()` (`src/main.js:820`) and the Stage Display's listeners (`src/stage.js:33-54`) fail at their first `await`. So slide-advanced, machine-state, mic-level, listening-stopped and download-progress never arrive. The tray item "Start / Stop listening" emits an event that nobody hears (`tray/mod.rs:35`). If the mic drops out, the screen still says "Listening".
- **Fix:** Add `src-tauri/capabilities/default.json` with `windows: ["main","stage"]` and `permissions: ["core:default"]`, about 8 lines.
- **If we skip it:** The operator and the worship leader see stale slides, and a dead mic shows no warning.

### 8. On most Windows microphones, listening will not start at all
- **What this is:** `AudioEngine::start` accepts only devices that can capture at exactly 16 kHz, and it does not convert other rates (`audio/mod.rs:73-95, 103-107`).
- **Problem:** On Windows, cpal 0.15.3 (the audio library) runs in shared mode. In that mode Windows accepts only the device's mix rate, usually 44.1 or 48 kHz, and cpal reports 16 kHz as unsupported (`cpal-0.15.3/src/host/wasapi/device.rs:149-172, 379-460`). So `start` fails with "does not support 16 kHz capture".
- **Fix:** Open the device at its default config and convert to 16 kHz mono in the audio callback. A simple linear resample of about 15 lines is enough for speech.
- **If we skip it:** On a typical Windows church laptop, the main feature does not work.

### 9. The "is running" flag gets stuck, or a restart quietly dies
- **What this is:** `start_listening_with_state` sets `is_running = true` and only then calls `audio.start()?` (`commands/mod.rs:173-195`).
- **Problem:** If `audio.start()` fails (finding 8, or an unplugged mic), the flag stays `true`, and every later Start says "Already running". If the operator presses stop and then quickly start again, the old loop is still busy transcribing. It wakes up, sees its audio channel closed, sets `is_running = false` (`:389-391`), and that ends the new session too. The check-then-set is also not atomic (two steps that two callers can interleave), so the HTTP API and the screen can both start at once.
- **Fix:** Use `compare_exchange(false, true)`, reset the flag on every error path, and give each session its own stop flag (`Arc<AtomicBool>`) instead of one shared flag.
- **If we skip it:** After one failed start, Voxxa will not listen until it is restarted. A quick stop and start can leave it silently not listening.

### 10. Any web page can control the local HTTP API
- **What this is:** The local HTTP API on 127.0.0.1 (`http_api/mod.rs:63-70`). The token is optional, and the screen sends `null` when the field is empty (`src/main.js:1560`, `http_api/mod.rs:116-119`).
- **Problem:** A web page open in any browser on the laptop can send `POST http://127.0.0.1:7575/api/v1/next` (or `/blank`, or `/listen/start`, which turns on the mic). Browsers send a plain POST like this without first asking permission (no CORS check). DNS rebinding, where an outside website makes the browser treat it as local, also lets a page read `/api/v1/state`. The server checks neither the `Origin` nor the `Host` header.
- **Fix:** Reject any request that has an `Origin` header, or whose `Host` is not `127.0.0.1:<port>` or `localhost:<port>`. Better: always require a token, created at random when the API is first turned on.
- **If we skip it:** A malicious ad or page in the operator's browser can blank or move slides, or turn on the mic.

### 11. Importing a bad PDF or a big file can freeze or crash the whole app
- **What this is:** `parse_song_text`, `parse_song_bytes` and `import_easyworship_db` are plain `pub fn` commands (`commands/mod.rs:989-1039`). Tauri runs non-async commands on the main thread, the one that also draws the window.
- **Problem:** A large PDF, pptx or EasyWorship database blocks the window until parsing ends. `pdf-extract` 0.8.2 has about 100 `unwrap`/`expect`/`panic!` sites in `src/lib.rs`, and nothing catches a panic (a Rust crash) here. A panic on the main thread inside the webview callback will most likely abort the whole process. I could not run this, so it is unverified.
- **Fix:** Make these commands `async` and run the parse inside `tauri::async_runtime::spawn_blocking`. Its `JoinError` turns a panic into an ordinary error.
- **If we skip it:** Adding one song during a service can kill Voxxa.

### 12. A broken model download is saved and shown as "Downloaded"
- **What this is:** `TranscriptionEngine::download_model` (`transcription/mod.rs:225-248`) and `is_model_downloaded`, which only checks that the file exists (`:95-97`).
- **Problem:** It writes straight to the final `ggml-*.bin` file. It never checks the HTTP status and never compares the size with Content-Length. A 404 page, or church Wi-Fi dropping halfway through 1.5 GB, leaves a bad file that the screen calls "Downloaded". Loading it then fails, and nothing in the screen lets the operator delete it.
- **Fix:** Call `error_for_status()`, write to `<name>.part`, check the byte count, and rename at the end.
- **If we skip it:** One failed download blocks that model until someone deletes the file by hand.

## Should fix

### 13. Song matching is slow, and the whole EasyWorship library becomes the setlist
- **What this is:** `partial_ratio` slides a window one character at a time and runs a full LCS (longest common subsequence: a character-by-character comparison) at each position (`aligner/mod.rs:507-566`). It does this for every song on every transcript (`:345-377`).
- **Problem:** I measured it in a scratch build with `rustc -O`: 72 ms for one song (190-character buffer against a 1,104-character song). Importing an EasyWorship database loads every song in the library as the setlist (`src/main.js:287-291`). With 500 songs, that is about 36 s per transcript, all on the one listening thread. The audio channel has no size limit (`audio/mod.rs:109`), so the delay and the memory use keep growing.
- **Fix:** Let the operator pick songs from the library instead of loading all of them. Also compare word by word, or move the window to word starts only, which means about 6 times fewer windows.
- **If we skip it:** With large setlists, slides move later and later, and memory keeps rising.

### 14. Long stretches of singing produce no text at all
- **What this is:** The voice detector (VAD) hands over a piece of audio only after 300 ms of silence (`vad/mod.rs:104-124`).
- **Problem:** If the singing has no 300 ms gap (worship over a band), the buffer keeps growing and Whisper gets nothing, so no slides move until the first pause. A related bug: `speech_samples` is never reset by silence before speech is confirmed (`:155-160`). So short noise blips spread out over time add up and count as speech.
- **Fix:** Hand over the buffer when it reaches about 5 s, even without a pause. Reset `speech_samples` on each silent frame while speech is not yet confirmed.
- **If we skip it:** Slides stay still for a whole song section, and then try to catch up.

### 15. No test covers the code that decides what reaches the screen
- **What this is:** `dispatch_action_with_total` (`commands/mod.rs:405-515`) has no tests. The aligner tests (`aligner/mod.rs:568-883`) never feed in speech that matches no song after a blank.
- **Problem:** Findings 1 and 2 both slipped through for this reason.
- **Fix:** Write one test with a fake presenter that has a toggle-style blank and records what was sent. Play song → silence → prayer speech → next song, and check the screen state (black or showing) after each step.
- **If we skip it:** The next change to this flow can bring the same failures back without anyone noticing.

### 16. The operator's view counts Next and Prev twice
- **What this is:** The Next and Prev buttons add or subtract 1 locally after the call returns (`src/main.js:736-757`). The `slide-advanced` handler also sets the index (`:864-869`).
- **Problem:** Once finding 7 is fixed, the event can arrive before the call returns. The handler then sets the new index, and the button adds 1 more, so the operator's view is one slide ahead of the real screen.
- **Fix:** Only change the index locally on the "no song" path (`Noop`), or drop the local change and rely on the event.
- **If we skip it:** The operator sees the wrong current slide and may "correct" a slide that was right.

### 17. Importing several files, one of them a setlist JSON, fails with no message
- **What this is:** `parseOneFile` returns the `parsed.setlist` array as if it were one song (`src/main.js:356`).
- **Problem:** The loop that renumbers slides (`:318-322`, outside any `try`) then hits `song.slides` on that array. It is undefined, so a TypeError stops the import and the operator sees nothing.
- **Fix:** In `importFiles`, flatten the array with `collected.push(...song)`.
- **If we skip it:** Dropping a setlist file together with extra songs silently does nothing.

### 18. Unused dependencies and dead code
- **What this is:** Code and packages that nothing uses.
  - `tauri-plugin-shell`, `-store`, `-global-shortcut`, `-updater` and `env_logger` (`Cargo.toml:22-26, 31`; `lib.rs:104-107`). No JavaScript calls them, and with no capability they cannot be called anyway.
  - The Python prototype: `main.py`, `voxxa/` and `requirements.txt`, about 336 lines (`README.md:63`).
  - `VadEngine::initialize` (`vad/mod.rs:79-83`) and `TranscriptionEngine::model_dir()` (`transcription/mod.rs:89-92`).
  - Fields that are never read: `TranscriptionResult.{language,duration_ms,is_partial}` and `Conductor.state_since`.
  - The `Arc<Mutex<Sender>>` wrapper (`audio/mod.rs:110`). `mpsc::Sender` is already `Send`.
- **Problem:** Every line here must be built, read and kept working for no benefit. The unused updater also gives a false sense that the app updates itself.
- **Fix:** Delete them. If auto-update is wanted, wire the updater up properly instead.
- **If we skip it:** Slower builds, more supply-chain surface, and confused readers.

### 19. Three copies of "blank", two of "stop" and three of "read state", which already disagree
- **What this is:** Blank plus `notify_external_blank` exists in three places: `tray/mod.rs:63-87`, `commands/mod.rs:747-759` and `http_api/mod.rs:199-215`. Stop exists in `http_api/mod.rs:230-245` and `commands/mod.rs:519-526`. State reading exists in `http_api/mod.rs:138-166`, `commands/mod.rs:530-554` and `diagnostics/mod.rs:108-123`.
- **Problem:** The copies already disagree. With no song yet, the HTTP API reports `current_slide: 0, is_blank: true`, while `get_status` reports `None`. Fixes such as finding 2 must be made three times.
- **Fix:** Write one `blank(state)`, one `stop(state)` and one status snapshot function in `commands`, and call them from every place.
- **If we skip it:** The next fix lands in one copy and not the others.

## Nice to have

### 20. Work that repeats on every transcript
- **What this is:** Each transcript re-joins and lowercases every song's text (`aligner/mod.rs:351-357`) and creates a new Whisper state (`transcription/mod.rs:166`).
- **Problem:** This repeats memory allocation for every song and the Whisper state setup every 5 s, for no change in result.
- **Fix:** Lowercase each song once in `Conductor::new`. Keep one `WhisperState` in the engine.
- **If we skip it:** A little wasted CPU on every transcript.
