## 0. Verdict

The ranking is right at the top and bloated below. **Agree:** no jump and no read-back is the first blocker; FreeShow is the only free fix; the parts map is the right follower; learning stays offline; the volunteer becomes a minder. **Change:** step 1 has a hidden half the report misses (voxxa cannot change *songs* on FreeShow today, finding 1), and the break-gated prior, learned resume prior, frame-level aligner (step 3) and forced-alignment Listener DB (step 5) are research bolted onto a plan that already has the measured mechanism. **Delete or defer them.** The bottleneck is founder hours (consent, recording, ~2 h tapping per Sunday) and a line budget with 1 line left in the row every product change needs. FreeShow's read-back removes most of the tapping: that is the strongest case for it, and the report does not make it.

## 2. Weakest evidence

1. **n = 1 song.** 74 of 77 is one singer, one room, one hymn, four re-cuts of the same take, a TTS fixture and live4 of the same hymn (measured). It beats the plan-order follower on that hymn; it says nothing about generalisation, and the report reads it as if it did.
2. **live4's truth is ±1 s** (FA-45 open) (measured). "5 of 16 early" and the parts map's "15 · 1 · 1" sit inside the truth's error.
3. **The break-gate premise fails on bands.** "59 of 63 jumps after a > 500 ms break" is clarinet practice (cited: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1512.07748); CODA is piano (cited: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21899). On P1 the detector found 2 of 11 breaks (measured): the gate is mute where jumps are hardest.
4. **0.82 s vs 0.071 s motivates nothing voxxa does**: it compares Whisper's *timestamp decoder* to CTC (cited: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2311.12488); voxxa times with onsets, never Whisper timestamps.
5. **FreeShow unknowns are under-listed.** Beyond index base and group ids (cited: https://freeshow.app/api): the driver says `can_query_state: false` (`freeshow.rs:87-91`), so "fire-then-verify" has no verify path; the song-change path (finding 1); Electron VRAM beside Whisper; whether PCO sync carries lyrics (ADR-026).
6. **Thresholds are proposals** (the report says so). One service ≈ 100 changes is ± 4 points (measured, spec § 2.3); four Sundays is the minimum for any 95 % claim.
7. **HCI transfer is thin** (crowd image labelling, cited: https://www.cise.ufl.edu/~eragan/papers/Honeycutt_HCOMP2020.pdf), but the conclusion is already ADR-023, so nothing hangs on it.

## 3. Code findings

### Must fix

1. **voxxa cannot change songs on FreeShow; it blanks instead** (`dispatch.rs:117-125`, `freeshow.rs:87-91`, `tests/dispatch.rs:289-305`). Goto drivers must show the right song before a goto; a driver that cannot report state is trusted only on operator actions. FreeShow cannot, so every conductor-driven first slide of a song sends `blank` and returns `Err("select …")`; the test pins it (measured). **Fix:** trait default `select_presentation(title) -> Result<bool>` (`Ok(false)`); FreeShow: `name_select_show {value}`; dispatch calls it on `new_song` before `presentation_is` (≈ 6 lines, wiring row, after V7); keep blank-and-ask on `false`. ADR-024 § 5 already routes song moves through driver methods. **Skip it:** FreeShow adds jumps and still needs a person at every song.
2. **The FreeShow goto very likely ignores its index (W1)** (`freeshow.rs:45-49`, `:166-170`). The driver nests fields under `data`; the docs and `api.ts` say `{action, ...data}` top-level (cited: https://freeshow.app/api). `index` is then absent (inferred). No wire test exists. **Fix:** merge `data` into the top level after § 5's probe confirms; a body-shape test on a `std::net::TcpListener` stub in `tests/common/`. **Skip it:** every later FreeShow number is wrong.
3. **The plan over-spends voxxa-eval** (`.10x/handoff.md:127`; V2 ≤ 100, V3b ≤ 20, V3d ≤ 25). 488/600 leaves 112; 145 planned (measured). **Fix:** V2 ≤ 70 (move `--model` passthrough to V3, truth-v2 event rows to V6); `tight_300` and `wrong_slide_s` share one signed-delay-per-truth-row helper, ≤ 15 lines together. voxxad is 48 left against 38 planned plus my `--watch` 10: no slack.
4. **A planned-next part is entered from mid-part on one window; the log then calls it a jump** (`parts.py:195`, `:277-279`, `:304-316`, `:320-323`). Paths into the planned next part carry `why='words'`; the margin and two-window gates apply only to `jump|lost`; the relabel to `jump` happens after the gates. Case: on slide 2 of a 4-slide verse, a window that fits the chorus's first slide (reachable when `stale`) moves on one window with no margin (inferred; no test can tell). **Fix:** relabel before the gate block, or gate any `y` that is neither `next_in_part(shown)` nor reached from the part's end. One pytest case. **Skip it:** a wrong slide with a log line that says it was meant.
5. **Elimination has no talk gate** (`parts.py:199-214`, `:293-299`). Speech is voiced: the leader prays 3 s after the chorus and `elim` shows the next verse (ADR-031 names the risk; the prototype has no mitigation; ADR-022's talk rules are in the product, not the lab). **Fix:** I don't know a cheap discriminator. Add "a spoken sentence after the chorus" to V3d task 4 and put `elim` behind a lab flag the live test may turn off. **Skip it:** a verse on screen during prayer.

## 4. What breaks under Sunday load

- **Pass time creeps past 1 s and the trigger goes quiet.** The keep-up gate drops hops when a hop behind (`listen.rs:159-166`); at 2.6 s passes the gain "mostly disappears" (measured). 0.53 s/pass was measured with Presenter open, not FreeShow (Electron) plus a stage display (measured). Add pass-time p95 to the session-end line and an amber above 1.0 s (V3).
- **Two apps, one projector input.** "One rehearsed song at a time" needs a per-song video switch (inferred; I don't know the switcher). Plan per service.
- **Talk after a chorus** (finding 5). **A second singer** on the feed: breaths stop lining up, a harmony line confuses which-part; no take has two voices (measured). Add one to V3d task 4.
- **Read-back latency**: getters poll up to 10 s (cited via the note's reading of api.ts); the driver's 3 s timeout inside dispatch delays the next move. Use 750 ms for read-back; a timeout is "unverified", never a failure.
- **Stage display in the stream**: next-slide text to the band is allowed (ADR-011 previews); captured into the stream it is not (inferred). FA-29 becomes a safety question.
- live.py's backlog (finding 7) and the front-window guard are Presenter-only; HTTP removes the latter, as the report says.
