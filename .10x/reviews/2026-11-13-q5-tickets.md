## Q5-01 [medium, tooling] Replace the stand-in fixtures with the founder's two public-domain hymn recordings
**What.** Both eval fixtures (`fixtures/recordings/prayer-then-reprise`, `prayer-without-pause`) are built from synthetic speech: Amazing Grace verses 1 and 2 and a prayer, read by the Windows SAPI voice (user decision (1), 2026-11-13). They are spoken, not sung, by one voice in a quiet room.
**Where.** `fixtures/recordings/`, `fixtures/SOURCES.md`, `fixtures/baseline.json`.
**Why.** The CI gate holds the pipeline steady, but its numbers say nothing about singing, a band, a hall or a congregation. Q6 conductor tuning against stand-ins alone could fit synthetic speech.
**Suggested fix.** Record the founder's two public-domain hymns with `fixtures/tools/record-session.ps1` (README "Recording a real fixture", including the hand-added first slide row and blank rows), convert to 16 kHz mono, put `audio.wav` on Drive, commit `setlist.json`, `truth.jsonl` and `session.jsonl` (`voxxa-eval --full --record fixtures/recordings/<name>`), add rows to `SOURCES.md`, and set `baseline.json` again by its rule (ADR-015 Decision 8). Keep or retire the stand-ins (they still cover the prayer shapes).

## Q5-02 [low, tooling, documentation] The recording kit has no blank key and writes no row for the first slide
**What.** `record-session.ps1` logs Enter (next slide) and `n` (next song) only. It writes no slide row for the slide on screen when recording starts (the first song is only on the `start` event line, which the eval skips), and the operator cannot mark a blank. Without a hand-added first row, every real recording scores a false advance on its first slide. The README's earlier step 5 pointed at `setlists/pd-hymns.json`, the older v1 setlist schema `voxxad` does not load (Q1 A15 follow-up); the README now says so.
**Where.** `fixtures/tools/record-session.ps1:63-85`; `fixtures/README.md` ("Recording a real fixture", step 5).
**Why.** Every real fixture needs truth rows added by hand, and blank timing (Q4-03) needs blank rows.
**Suggested fix.** Add a `b` + Enter key that writes `{"t_ms":…,"blank":true}`, and write `{"t_ms":0,"song":<title>,"slide":0}` at start. Longer term, `voxxad --log-keys` (P1.6) or GUI operator inputs in the session log (Q3-09, #53) should write this `truth.jsonl` shape (ADR-015 Decision 5).

## Q5-03 [low, tooling] `voxxa-eval` may be bundled into the installers
**What.** Like `voxxad` (Q3-03), the new `src/bin/voxxa-eval/` target is built by `cargo build`, and `tauri build` probably bundles every binary target.
**Where.** `src-tauri/Cargo.toml` (no `[[bin]]` entry or `required-features` for `voxxa-eval`).
**Why.** A developer tool in the church installer adds a second unsigned executable on macOS and Windows.
**Suggested fix.** Decide with Q3-03: `required-features = ["cli"]` on both tool binaries (CI and `cargo test` then pass `--features cli`), or a bundler exclusion. Confirm with `tauri build --no-bundle`.

## Q5-04 [medium] A song that starts after an opening prayer is not shown until its next verse
**What.** In the 10x lane's fixture `standin-prayer-between` (1 s silence, an opening prayer, 2 s, Amazing Grace verse 1, the prayer again, 1 s, verse 2) Voxxa never shows verse 1; the first slide shown is verse 2, 6.7 s after it starts (recall 0.5 on that fixture). Verified cause: before any song is committed the conductor keeps the prayer's words in its 40-word buffer, so the buffer does not read as the song. Replaying the same log with the prayer's `hypothesis` lines (t < 10 s) removed shows verse 1 at 14,000 ms, 2.8 s after it starts.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:370-411` (`on_transcript`: the buffer and the `current_song == None` start path).
**Reproduction.** Commit `30e8990` on `q5/10x` holds the fixture: `fixtures/recordings/standin-prayer-between/{audio.json,setlist.json,session.jsonl,truth.jsonl}`. Its `truth.jsonl` uses the 10x lane's blank row `{"t_ms":23200,"kind":"blank"}`; under the merged eval write it as `{"t_ms":23200,"blank":true}`. Then replay, `voxxa-eval <that folder>`, prints (checked on `q5/base`): slides 2, gotos 1, hits 1, missed 1, recall 0.50, latency 6,710 ms, blank `miss`, unblank `miss` (and exits 1, since the folder has no baseline row).
**Why.** An opening prayer or welcome before the first song is the normal shape of a service; the first verse of the first song would stay dark.
**Suggested fix.** Q6: before a song is committed, score the start on the newest words only (as the re-show path already does with `text`), or clear the buffer after a stretch of unmatched speech. When Q6 fixes it, add this shape as a third fixture in the same commit, with its `baseline.json` row and an updated `total`. It is not added in Q5: today Voxxa never blanks into that prayer, so its blank is `miss` and its row would force `blank_ms` off the `total` row.

## Q5-05 [low, performance, tooling] Debug replay of the fixtures takes about 27 s per OS
**What.** `voxxa-eval` in replay mode (debug build) takes about 27 s on the reference PC for both stand-in fixtures (about 93 s of audio), mostly the conductor's fuzzy scoring on every transcript and tick, unoptimised. `cargo test` runs it twice more (`replay_of_the_committed_fixtures_passes_the_baseline`, `a_broken_threshold_fails_the_gate_and_names_the_metric`), and the CI eval step once, on each OS.
**Where.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (step "voxxa-eval (fixtures vs baseline)"); `src-tauri/tests/voxxa_eval.rs`; `src-tauri/src/aligner/` scoring (Q4-05 measures the scoring cost per setlist size).
**Why.** More and longer fixtures (a real service is an hour) will make the gate slow in debug.
**Suggested fix.** Measure where the time goes first. Options: `[profile.dev.package.voxxa] opt-level = 1` for the CI eval step only, or run the eval step on a `--release` build once Q4-01 is fixed.

## Q5-07 [low, tooling] Replay cannot measure a change to a `SmartConfig` default
**What.** `voxxad --replay` rebuilds the conductor from the `SmartConfig` stored in each log's start line (`info.config`), not from today's defaults. A conductor-logic change is measured by replay and CI, but a change to a `SmartConfig` default (a threshold, a debounce) is invisible until the logs are re-recorded with Whisper or their start-line config is edited by hand.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxad/events.rs:213-215`; `fixtures/README.md` (Replay bullet); ADR-015 Consequences.
**Why.** Q6 tunes the conductor, and much of that tuning is `SmartConfig` defaults; the CI gate would not see it.
**Suggested fix.** For Q6: a `--current-config` option (about 10 lines) on `voxxad --replay` that uses `SmartConfig::default()` instead of the log's, passed through by `voxxa-eval` (or always used by it). Not built in Q5.
