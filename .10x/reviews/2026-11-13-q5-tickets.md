## Q5-01 [medium, tooling] Replace the stand-in fixtures with the founder's two public-domain hymn recordings
**What.** Both eval fixtures (`fixtures/recordings/prayer-then-reprise`, `prayer-without-pause`) are built from synthetic speech: Amazing Grace verses 1 and 2 and a prayer, read by the Windows SAPI voice (user decision (1), 2026-11-13). They are spoken, not sung, by one voice in a quiet room.
**Where.** `fixtures/recordings/`, `fixtures/SOURCES.md`, `fixtures/baseline.json`.
**Why.** The CI gate holds the pipeline steady, but its numbers say nothing about singing, a band, a hall or a congregation. Q6 conductor tuning against stand-ins alone could fit synthetic speech.
**Suggested fix.** Record the founder's two public-domain hymns with `fixtures/tools/record-session.ps1` (README "Recording a real fixture", including the hand-added first slide row and blank rows), convert to 16 kHz mono, put `audio.wav` on Drive, commit `setlist.json`, `truth.jsonl` and `session.jsonl` (`voxxa-eval --full --record fixtures/recordings/<name>`), add rows to `SOURCES.md`, and set `baseline.json` again by its rule (ADR-015 Decision 8). Keep or retire the stand-ins (they still cover the prayer shapes).

## Q5-02 [low, tooling, documentation] The recording kit has no blank key and writes no row for the first slide
**What.** `record-session.ps1` logs Enter (next slide) and `n` (next song) only. It writes no slide row for the slide on screen when recording starts (the first song is only on the `start` event line, which the eval skips), and the operator cannot mark a blank. Without a hand-added first row, every real recording scores a false advance on its first slide. The README's earlier step 5 pointed at `setlists/pd-hymns.json`, the older v1 setlist schema `voxxad` does not load (Q1 A15 follow-up); the README now says so.
**Where.** `fixtures/tools/record-session.ps1:63-85`; `fixtures/README.md` ("Recording a real fixture", step 5).
**Why.** Every real fixture needs truth rows added by hand, and blank timing (Q4-03) needs blank rows.
**Suggested fix.** Add a `b` + Enter key that writes `{"t_ms":…,"blank":true}`, and write `{"t_ms":0,"song":<title>,"slide":0}` at start. Longer term, `voxxad --log-keys` (P1.6) or GUI operator inputs in the session log (Q3-09, #53) should write this `truth.jsonl` shape (ADR-015 Decision 5).

## Q5-04 [medium] A song that starts after an opening prayer is not shown until its next verse
**What.** In the 10x lane's fixture `standin-prayer-between` (1 s silence, an opening prayer, 2 s, Amazing Grace verse 1, the prayer again, 1 s, verse 2) Voxxa never shows verse 1; the first slide shown is verse 2, 6.7 s after it starts (recall 0.5 on that fixture). Verified cause: before any song is committed the conductor keeps the prayer's words in its 40-word buffer, so the buffer does not read as the song. Replaying the same log with the prayer's `hypothesis` lines (t < 10 s) removed shows verse 1 at 14,000 ms, 2.8 s after it starts.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:370-411` (`on_transcript`: the buffer and the `current_song == None` start path).
**Reproduction.** Commit `30e8990` on `q5/10x` holds the fixture: `fixtures/recordings/standin-prayer-between/{audio.json,setlist.json,session.jsonl,truth.jsonl}`. Its `truth.jsonl` uses the 10x lane's blank row `{"t_ms":23200,"kind":"blank"}`; under the merged eval write it as `{"t_ms":23200,"blank":true}`. Then replay, `voxxa-eval <that folder>`, prints (checked on `q5/base`): slides 2, gotos 1, hits 1, missed 1, recall 0.50, latency 6,710 ms, blank `miss`, unblank `miss` (and exits 1, since the folder has no baseline row).
**Why.** An opening prayer or welcome before the first song is the normal shape of a service; the first verse of the first song would stay dark.
**Suggested fix.** Q6: before a song is committed, score the start on the newest words only (as the re-show path already does with `text`), or clear the buffer after a stretch of unmatched speech. When Q6 fixes it, add this shape as a third fixture in the same commit, with its `baseline.json` row and an updated `total`. It is not added in Q5: today Voxxa never blanks into that prayer, so its blank is `miss` and its row would force `blank_ms` off the `total` row.

## Q5-05 [low, performance, tooling] Debug replay of the fixtures takes about 27 s per OS
**What.** `voxxa-eval` in replay mode (debug build) takes about 27 s on the reference PC for both stand-in fixtures (about 93 s of audio; verifier A measured 18-24 s per fixture on a loaded PC), mostly the conductor's fuzzy scoring on every transcript and tick, unoptimised. `cargo test` runs it three more times (`replay_of_the_committed_fixtures_passes_the_baseline`, `a_broken_threshold_fails_the_gate_and_names_the_metric`, `a_broken_per_fixture_threshold_fails_the_gate_while_total_passes`), and the CI eval step once, on each OS. Adds to Q3-13 (#57) and Q4-10 (#68).
**Where.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (step "voxxa-eval (fixtures vs baseline)"); `src-tauri/tests/voxxa_eval.rs`; `src-tauri/src/aligner/` scoring (Q4-05 measures the scoring cost per setlist size).
**Why.** More and longer fixtures (a real service is an hour) will make the gate slow in debug.
**Suggested fix.** Measure where the time goes first. Options: `[profile.dev.package.voxxa] opt-level = 1` for the CI eval step only, or run the eval step on a `--release` build once Q4-01 is fixed.

## Q5-07 [low, tooling] Replay cannot measure a change to a `SmartConfig` default
**What.** `voxxad --replay` rebuilds the conductor from the `SmartConfig` stored in each log's start line (`info.config`), not from today's defaults. A conductor-logic change is measured by replay and CI, but a change to a `SmartConfig` default (a threshold, a debounce) is invisible until the logs are re-recorded with Whisper or their start-line config is edited by hand.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxad/events.rs:213-215`; `fixtures/README.md` (Replay bullet); ADR-015 Consequences.
**Why.** Q6 tunes the conductor, and much of that tuning is `SmartConfig` defaults; the CI gate would not see it.
**Suggested fix.** For Q6: a `--current-config` option (about 10 lines) on `voxxad --replay` that uses `SmartConfig::default()` instead of the log's, passed through by `voxxa-eval` (or always used by it). Not built in Q5.

### Q5-08 (closed in 5858872; not filed) No test covers an action before the first truth row's window (verifier B, M65)
**Closed.** The orchestrator added the suggested case to `the_span_boundaries_are_exact_to_the_millisecond`, applied the mutant by hand and saw the test fail. Original ticket text follows.
**What.** ADR-015 Decision 6 says an action before `t_0 − 1000` belongs to no row and is a false advance. No test pins it. With the row lookup changed to fall back to row 0 (`truth.iter().rposition(|r| r.t <= a.t + EARLY_MS).or(Some(0))`), all 26 `voxxa_eval` tests pass: an early goto to the first slide then counts as a hit with negative latency. The only first-row test puts the goto exactly on the boundary (truth at 1,000, goto at 0).
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxa-eval/score.rs:80-81` (the row lookup); `src-tauri/tests/voxxa_eval.rs:126` (`the_span_boundaries_are_exact_to_the_millisecond`).
**Why.** A natural refactor (`partition_point(..).saturating_sub(1)`) behaves exactly like the mutant. It would score a first slide shown during an opening prayer as a hit, hiding the Q5-04 shape that Q6 is meant to fix and measure. **Blocks the Q5 merge** (verifier B).
**Suggested fix.** Add next to the span-boundary test: `let m = score(&[slide(2100, 0)], &[slide(1000, 0)]); assert_eq!((m.hits, m.false_advances), (0, 1));`. Run it against the mutant above and see it fail.

## Q5-10 [low, tests] Two small survivors in `voxxa-eval` remain (verifier B, M57, M68)
**Status.** M52 and M55 were closed in 5858872 (an unknown action kind is now asserted to be an error; blank, blank, goto times the unblank at the goto and counts one double); each mutant was applied by hand and fails the test. M57 and M68 are deferred: M57's check works and CI has no model, so a replay that ran Whisper fails there anyway; M68 only changes a stderr message. Neither is an exit criterion.
**What.**
- **M52:** `parse_actions` silently skipping an unknown action kind (`_ => continue` instead of `_ => return Err(bad())`) passes every test. A new `voxxad` action kind, or a typo, would be dropped from scoring without notice.
- **M55:** timing the unblank at the next action of any kind (`.next()` instead of `.find(|a| a.cue != Cue::Blank)`) passes. With `voxxad` output blank, blank, goto, the unblank would be timed at the second blank.
- **M57:** deleting the replay/full mode check (`if false && …`) passes. The check works (a replay that runs Whisper exits 2), but no test pins it; locally, with the model present, such a replay would print `mode: replay` and could pass. CI has no model and would fail anyway.
- **M68:** dropping the stderr report of `voxxad`'s `error` lines passes (cosmetic).
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxa-eval/score.rs:315` (M52) and `:107` (M55); `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxa-eval/main.rs:97-98` (M57) and `:101` (M68).
**Why.** Each lets a scoring or reporting rule regress silently. M52 and M55 are one-line tests and should go in with Q5-08 and Q5-09 before the merge; M57 and M68 may be deferred with a recorded reason.
**Suggested fix.**
- M52: add `assert!(parse_actions(r#"{"v":1,"t":1,"type":"action","action":"next"}"#).is_err());` to `actions_are_read_from_voxxad_stdout_and_other_lines_are_skipped`.
- M55: add to `blank_and_unblank_are_timed_from_the_operator_rows`: actions `[blank(25500), blank(30000), slide(42500, 1)]` give unblank `[Some(2500)]` and `double_advances` 1.
- M57: factor the check into a small pure function and unit-test it with a stdout that holds a `start` status line while replay was asked for.
- M68: optional, low value.

## Q5-11 [low, tests, documentation] Scorer edge cases left as known gaps
**What.**
- Explicit `unblank` actions are never counted as false or double advances, and an early `unblank` gives a negative `unblank_ms`, which counts as better. An `unblank` in the middle of a prayer passes every gate. Pinned by `an_unblank_during_a_prayer_is_neither_false_nor_double` and documented; neither committed log has an `unblank`.
- Two truth rows with the same `t_ms` make the earlier one unreachable (the lookup takes the latest row at or before `a.t + 1000`); a hand-written typo shows up as a miss.
- A `baseline.json` row for a fixture that no longer exists is ignored (verifier A; documented).
- The `total` row assumes the full fixture set; running a subset changes it and may fail it (documented).
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxa-eval/score.rs:70-73` (unblank), `:80` (row lookup), `:222` (`check`); `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxa-eval/main.rs` (baseline rows); `fixtures/README.md`.
**Why.** None of these is hit by today's fixtures or conductor, but each can mis-score a real recording or a Q6 change without failing the gate.
**Suggested fix.** Count an `unblank` on a screen that is not blank as a double, and one more than 1 s before the next truth slide row as a false advance. Reject equal `t_ms` on consecutive truth rows in `parse_truth`. Fail (exit 2) on a baseline row that names no fixture when the eval runs the full set. Add a hand-computed test for each.

## Q5-12 [low, documentation] Small wording and message gaps found by verifier A
**What.**
- `fixtures/README.md` ("The current fixtures") and ADR-015 Decision 10 say truth rows are at each part's start plus 100 ms. The real offsets are +100 to +135 ms, rounded to 100 ms (16,100 for a part at 15,995; 30,400 for 30,280; 40,500 for 40,365).
- FAIL lines print ratios with two decimals, so a precision of 0.995 against a threshold of 1.0 would print `precision is 1.00, baseline needs >= 1`.
**Where.** `fixtures/README.md`; `.10x/adrs/015-voxxa-eval-fixtures-and-replay-gate.md` Decision 10; `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxa-eval/score.rs:248` (the FAIL message).
**Why.** A reader re-deriving the truth times, or reading a FAIL line, gets a slightly wrong picture.
**Suggested fix.** Say "the part's start plus the measured speech onset, rounded up to 100 ms" in both places. Print ratios with three decimals in FAIL lines (the table can keep two).
