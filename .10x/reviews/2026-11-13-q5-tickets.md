## Q5-01 [medium, tooling] Replace the stand-in fixtures with the founder's two public-domain hymn recordings
**What.** Both eval fixtures (`fixtures/recordings/standin-*`) are built from synthetic speech: Amazing Grace verses 1 and 2 and a prayer, read by the Windows SAPI voice (user decision (1), 2026-11-13). They are spoken, not sung, by one voice in a quiet room.
**Where.** `fixtures/recordings/`, `fixtures/SOURCES.md`, `fixtures/baseline.json`.
**Why.** The CI gate holds the pipeline steady, but its numbers say nothing about singing, a band, a hall or a congregation. Q6 conductor tuning against stand-ins alone could fit synthetic speech.
**Suggested fix.** Record the founder's two public-domain hymns with `fixtures/tools/record-session.ps1` (README "Recording a real fixture"), convert to 16 kHz mono, put `audio.wav` on Drive, commit `setlist.json`, `truth.jsonl` and `session.jsonl` (`voxxa-eval --full --model tiny --record`), add rows to `SOURCES.md`, and set `baseline.json` again by its rule. Keep or retire the stand-ins (they still cover the prayer shapes).

## Q5-02 [low, tooling, documentation] The recording kit has no blank key and writes no row for the first slide
**What.** `record-session.ps1` logs Enter (next slide) and `n` (next song) only. It writes no row for the slide on screen when recording starts, and the operator cannot mark a blank. Its step 5 pointed at `setlists/pd-hymns.json`, the v1 schema `voxxad` does not load (Q1 A15 follow-up); the README now says so.
**Where.** `fixtures/tools/record-session.ps1:63-85`; `fixtures/README.md` ("Recording a real fixture").
**Why.** Every real fixture needs truth rows added by hand, and blank timing (Q4-03) needs blank rows.
**Suggested fix.** Add a `b` + Enter key that writes `{"t_ms":…,"kind":"blank"}`, and write `{"t_ms":0,"song":<title>,"slide":0}` at start. Longer term, `voxxad --log-keys` (P1.6) or GUI operator inputs in the session log (Q3-09, #53) should write this `truth.jsonl` shape.

## Q5-03 [low, tooling] `voxxa-eval` may be bundled into the installers
**What.** Like `voxxad` (Q3-03), the new `src/bin/voxxa-eval/` target is built by `cargo build`, and `tauri build` probably bundles every binary target.
**Where.** `src-tauri/Cargo.toml` (no `[[bin]]` entry or `required-features` for `voxxa-eval`).
**Why.** A developer tool in the church installer adds a second unsigned executable on macOS and Windows.
**Suggested fix.** Decide with Q3-03: `required-features = ["cli"]` on both tool binaries (CI and `cargo test` then pass `--features cli`), or a bundler exclusion. Confirm with `tauri build --no-bundle`.

## Q5-04 [medium] A song that starts after an opening prayer is not shown until its next verse
**What.** In `standin-prayer-between` (opening prayer, then Amazing Grace verse 1) Voxxa never shows verse 1; the first slide shown is verse 2, 6.7 s after it starts (recall 0.5 on that fixture). Verified cause: before any song is committed the conductor keeps the prayer's words in its 40-word buffer, so the buffer does not read as the song. Replaying the same log with the prayer's `hypothesis` lines (t < 10 s) removed shows verse 1 at 14,000 ms, 2.8 s after it starts.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:370-411` (`on_transcript`: the buffer and the `current_song == None` start path); `fixtures/recordings/standin-prayer-between/session.jsonl`.
**Why.** An opening prayer or welcome before the first song is the normal shape of a service; the first verse of the first song would stay dark.
**Suggested fix.** Q6: before a song is committed, score the start on the newest words only (as the re-show path already does with `text`), or clear the buffer after a stretch of unmatched speech. Measure with `voxxa-eval`; tighten `fixtures/baseline.json` (recall) in the same commit.

## Q5-05 [low, performance, tooling] Replay of a 45 s fixture takes 10-30 s in a debug build
**What.** `voxxad --replay` on a stand-in log takes 9-30 s in debug on the reference PC (the conductor's fuzzy scoring on every transcript and tick, unoptimised). The CI eval step adds about a minute per OS.
**Where.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (step "voxxa-eval (replay gate)"); `src-tauri/src/aligner/` scoring (Q4-05 measures the scoring cost per setlist size).
**Why.** More and longer fixtures (a real service is an hour) will make the gate slow in debug.
**Suggested fix.** Measure where the time goes first. Options: `[profile.dev.package.voxxa] opt-level = 1` for the CI eval step only, or run the eval step on a `--release` build once Q4-01 is fixed.
