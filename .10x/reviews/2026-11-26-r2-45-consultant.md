## 0. Summary

The report is right that the first blocker is the output side, and right that the ministry team must become
minders rather than watchers. It is wrong about what the FreeShow move costs, because it counts the cost in
engineering (W1, the driver, the song re-import) and not in Sundays. From the church's side the move is a change
of presentation software for the whole service, done by volunteers, during the only hour that matters, with a
repertoire that shrinks to what can be re-imported from licensed sources. None of that is in `plan.md`: there is
no church-side migration step, no reversible fallback, no minder programme, and the kit still assumes
WorshipTools and a person at the PC. The six-step ranking should keep FreeShow first but run the minder work from
week one (it is needed for every shadow Sunday regardless of app), pull the input chain ahead of the parts-map
refinements (three of four live failures were setup), and park the aligner and the reference tracker until a
second church runs songs mode. The two gates the report and the kit disagree on (two or four Sundays) must be
split into two different promises before anyone tells the team anything.

## 1. Findings, ranked by severity

**F1 (critical). The migration is a service-wide software change, not a library re-import.** The report says
"the migration cost sits almost entirely in the song library" (report § Presenter's keystroke ceiling). From the
church side the costs are: (a) the slide volunteers learn a new app; (b) every announcement, scripture, sermon
slide and media item is rebuilt, because voxxa does none of those and they live in Presenter today; (c) the
streaming person's capture path may change (I don't know whether the stream captures Presenter's output window,
an HDMI split, or something else; the kit intake does not ask); (d) the worship leader's stage display changes
(Presenter's is a paid plan, FreeShow's is free (cited: https://www.worshiptools.com/en-us/presenter;
https://freeshow.app/docs/stage)); (e) Planning Center: FreeShow pulls plans a week ahead (cited:
https://freeshow.app/docs/integrations), but voxxa's own service plan is still typed by hand
(`kit/prompts/03-layout.md` step 3), so the team now keeps three copies of the order: PCO, FreeShow, voxxa. (f)
Two presentation apps cannot own the projector at once, so "switch the live output for one rehearsed song at a
time" (report § Presenter's keystroke ceiling) means two output windows and a display swap mid-set, which is the
kind of thing that produces a black projector in front of the congregation (inferred). Recommendation: switch per
service, never per song; build the service in both apps for a fixed number of "double-build Sundays"; rollback is
a decision taken by Thursday evening, not a live swap.

**F2 (high). The repertoire shrinks, and nobody has said so to the worship leader.** The brief and ADR-026 allow
only CC, public-domain, church-owned or permission songs (CCLI legacy, optional). WorshipTools has no bulk export
(cited: https://worshiptools.com/help/thread/6276188217540608). So the FreeShow library is rebuilt from
licensed sources only, and a song with no licensed source is not re-imported. In a small church the Sunday
rotation is mostly contemporary CCLI-licensed songs (opinion). That makes the migration a repertoire decision owned
by the worship leader and pastor, not the tech lead. It must be signed off before any migration weekend, with a
count: how many songs in the current rotation have a licensed source, how many do not, and what happens to the
latter (dropped, kept in Presenter during the transition, or re-licensed). The kit's `songs.csv` already carries
the fields; what is missing is the inventory step and the sign-off.

**F3 (high). The minder programme is specified for a product that does not exist yet.** The report's minder
(report § Volunteers become minders) needs a reserved hold/kill key, a stage display with provisional/firm
colours, a Companion button on stage, a health-check screen and a mis-cued replay for training. Today the product
has Simple mode with a status light and a Call-for-help button (`r2-review/docs/quick-start.md`), Pause is
planned for V14 (F15 from a pedal), the Run screen is a mock (V10), and the health check is a PowerShell ffmpeg
line in a checklist (`church-setup-checklist.md` § 8). The role card must therefore exist in two versions:
v0 for what ships through the shadow Sundays (WorshipTools, Presenter in front, Pause via the pedal once V13/V14
land, "put the song live" amber cards), and v1 for FreeShow with read-back. Training people on v1 before v0 exists
loses them (opinion).

**F4 (high). Two different Sunday counts are being promised.** The kit says songs mode after two consecutive
shadow Sundays meeting the Correct bar (`kit/prompts/07-review.md`; spec § 7.6); the report says "the church
runs without a volunteer only after four consecutive shadow Sundays" (report § Acceptance). These are different
promises: (i) voxxa may move slides while a minder sits at the PC; (ii) the minder may leave the PC. From the
church's side both are needed and they must be told apart. Proposed: two scored shadow Sundays → songs mode with
the minder at the PC; four consecutive green songs-mode Sundays with no hold and no manual Next → the minder is
"on call in the room" (FA-48 below). Trust is lost faster by invisible nothing than by visible small mistakes:
shadow mode shows the team nothing for weeks. Pair every shadow Sunday with one number shared with the team on
Monday ("voxxa would have got 47 of 49 slides") (opinion; consistent with the trust-calibration finding that
showing capability calibrates trust (cited: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02114)).

**F5 (high). Scoring a shadow Sunday costs the founder about two hours of tapping per Sunday, and the plan has
no other tapper.** FA-06 budgets "~2 h of tap-truth"; prompt 07 requires a desk recording, intake, tap-truth,
offline replay and `voxxa-eval --actions` for every scored Sunday. The founder is the single point of failure the
brief names, and this is where the hours go. The report's confirm-or-nudge tiering (report § Self-training) is
not built. Two cheap fixes: (a) the operator notes talk-span times live from the service plan, so talk is muted
before anyone taps, and a consented volunteer taps songs only; (b) score a rotating subset (three songs per
Sunday) until the tiering exists, and say in prompt 07 that a partially scored Sunday counts for the songs scored.
Both are kit changes, no code.

**F6 (medium). Rehearsal-as-practice is under-specified on the human side.** Who presses record, who signs, what
happens with a guest musician, and what the worship leader does at the end are not in prompt 05. Reality: guest
singers and swapped songs are weekly events in a small church (opinion). The kit's rule "no consent → stop" is
right, but the card must say what "stop" means (no learning this week; voxxa still follows on Sunday with cold
rules). The confirm step (`voxxad --learn` prints part labels; the leader says yes per song) must happen before
the band leaves, so it belongs to the minder on rota, not the leader, and takes about three minutes for six songs
(inferred from the checklist's one-line-per-song output). A Sunday-morning arrangement change is what the parts
map is for (measured: F1–F4 re-cuts, 42 of 44 right); a Sunday-morning song swap is not in the setlist and voxxa
only prompts the operator (spec § 6), so the card says: a new song is this month's manual song. Medleys and
spontaneous repeats of a single line: I don't know how the follower behaves; the card says "hold".

**F7 (medium). Scripture, announcements and media are out of scope, so the pitch to the pastor must be
"nobody at the PC during the worship set", not "nobody at the PC".** FA-34 keeps scripture and cue graphics
suggest-only in v1; Presenter graphics are hints. Someone still runs the sermon. Say it that way from day one or
the pastor hears a promise voxxa does not make (opinion).

**F8 (medium). Congregational scepticism and incidental recording.** American Christians are more sceptical of
AI in services (cited: https://foxnews.com/politics/christians-skeptical-ai-tech-churches.amp). Recommend the
word "AI" is never used from the stage or in the bulletin; the description is "the slides follow the singer".
The consent form covers singers and speakers; a vocal-mic feed also carries the congregation faintly. I don't
know the law; the kit should say "ask counsel whether a general notice is needed" and offer a one-paragraph
notice template.

**F9 (medium). Feature creep is already in the report.** Steps 3 (frame-level aligner, CTC, keyword spotting),
the reference-recording OLTW tracker and singer adaptation are lab programmes. None changes what a volunteer does.
Recommend the founder refuses them until a second church runs songs mode; the brief's ADR-028 "new module gets a
new row" rule is the right brake.

**F10 (low). Kit gaps for FreeShow and the minder** (details in § 4 and § 5): no FreeShow rows on the test card
beyond T12; `church-profile.json` has no fields for stage display, stream capture path or scripture source; the
service-day checklist assumes keys-only; no role card, drill, rota, congregation note or worship-leader card;
`06-live-readiness.md` step 2 "the presentation app's window is in front" is wrong for FreeShow.

## 2. What each person calls "it works", and what happens the first time it does not

- **Pastor:** nobody is recruited for slides in the worship set; no visible slide mistake he is asked about after
  the service; no licensing letter; nothing about AI said from the stage; the stream did not go black.
- **Worship leader:** the slide is never ahead of the singer (measured bar: 0 moves earlier than 0.3 s in the
  promotion pass, as the report proposes); repeats and skips are followed (measured: parts map 74 of 77 in the
  lab); one gesture stops it without looking at the booth; and he is told in advance that the verse after a chorus
  will arrive one to three seconds after its first word (measured: ADR-031 cost) so he does not read it as a
  failure. The last point is the veto moment: if the leader hates the post-chorus wait, `boundary = plan` per
  leader from practice memory is the answer, and it needs V12.
- **Volunteer (minder):** the card is one page; Sunday costs ten minutes before and nothing during; one button
  when it goes wrong; and nobody blames them for a machine's mistake.
- **Congregation (proxy):** wrong-slide seconds under 10 per service (report's budget), no slide flashing (no Left
  bursts), no blank mid-song (ADR-022 gate).

**How many Sundays before trust is lost:** about three Sundays of visible failure, or six to eight weeks of
invisible nothing (opinion). Hence the per-song promotion and the Monday number.

**The first wrong slide:** the minder presses Hold (today: Pause/F15 when V14 lands; before that, clicks the
right slide in the presenter), fixes the slide by hand, writes the song position and the slide numbers on the
card; the song is demoted to suggest-only (report's rule, ADR-022's "operator wins"); the worship leader hears one
sentence on Monday, not an apology from the stage; the Monday review (prompt 07) turns it into a numbers-only note.
This protocol belongs on the card, because the instinct of a volunteer is to press Next three times (opinion).

## 7. Founder decisions (FA items), with recommendations

- **FA-46 — Migration shape.** (a) per-service switch with four double-build Sundays and a Thursday-18:00
  rollback rule; (b) per-song switch as the report says; (c) big-bang on one Sunday. **Recommend (a).** Two apps
  never own the projector in one service.
- **FA-47 — Repertoire sign-off.** The worship leader signs the inventory counts (licensed source / none /
  legacy CCLI) before any migration weekend, and decides what happens to songs without a source. **Recommend
  yes;** target: the first ten re-imported songs are the ten most-sung licensed songs.
- **FA-48 — Two gates, not one.** (a) two scored shadow Sundays → songs mode with the minder at the PC; four
  consecutive green songs-mode Sundays (no hold, no manual Next, wrong-slide seconds under budget) → the minder is
  on call in the room; (b) the report's four shadow Sundays for both. **Recommend (a).** Shadow mode is invisible
  to the team; the second gate is the one that actually removes the person.
- **FA-49 — Who minds.** Minimum three minders from the existing slide team first; the founder is never the
  Sunday minder from month two; one veteran (three months) trains the next. **Recommend yes.**
- **FA-50 — Words.** No "AI" from the stage or in the bulletin; a one-paragraph notice reviewed by counsel.
  **Recommend yes;** the pastor decides the wording.
- **FA-51 — Tapping labour.** The operator notes talk spans live; talk is muted; a consented volunteer taps songs
  only; partial scoring counts. **Recommend yes.** It cuts the founder's Sunday cost from about two hours to under
  one.
- **FA-52 — Stage display and veto surface.** (a) FreeShow's stage view on an existing phone or tablet over the
  booth network, veto by the booth pedal only in v1; (b) a Companion button on stage (hardware purchase).
  **Recommend (a) now, (b) when a church asks.**
- **FA-53 — Whole service or worship set.** (a) the whole service moves to FreeShow on the first live Sunday,
  with the announcement and scripture slides rebuilt beforehand; (b) only the worship set, Presenter for the rest.
  **Recommend (a).** (b) means two apps in one service.

## 8. Risks the report missed

1. **Display ownership and the stream.** Two presentation apps cannot both drive the projector; a live swap is a
   black screen; the stream capture path may be Presenter-specific. (inferred; the kit must ask.)
2. **GPU contention is a visible failure, not a lab number.** FreeShow is an Electron app with GPU compositing;
   the GTX 960 drives the projector, Whisper (814 of 2048 MB measured with Presenter open) and now FreeShow. The
   report asks for a VRAM measurement; the church sees it as a video stutter during the pre-service loop. Measure
   with a video playing. (inferred)
3. **The worship leader's reaction to the post-chorus wait** (1–3 s measured) is the single most likely reason
   the leader asks to turn it off. Tell them first; have `boundary = plan` per leader ready (V12).
4. **The slide volunteers lose a job.** Frame the minder role as an upgrade to the people who hold the role today,
   or the tech team shrinks before the machine is ready (opinion; consistent with the retention sources in the
   human-factors note).
5. **Calendar.** The Christmas freeze (Dec 18 – Jan 3), Lent and Easter (Mar 28, 2027) bound the migration
   window to roughly Jan 11 – Mar 21, 2027; a slip of three weeks pushes it past Easter into the summer, when
   small-church volunteer attendance drops (opinion).
6. **FreeShow's own updates.** An Electron app may update itself and change its API or ports; pin the version
   after the final rehearsal (the report's own QLab lesson) and add "FreeShow version" to the service-day
   checklist. I don't know whether FreeShow auto-updates.
7. **If the founder is also the worship leader** (T1 is the founder's own voice (measured)), the "stage veto"
   and the "booth minder" collapse into one person on Sundays, and the founder's time cap is fiction. I don't
   know the church's staffing; FA-49 should state it.
8. **Incidental recording of the congregation** through the vocal mic bleed; the consent form does not cover it.
   Counsel.
9. **Three copies of the service order** (PCO, FreeShow, voxxa) until Q12 read-back lands; the keys-only "nothing
   between two songs" rule disappears with FreeShow, but the three-copies drift replaces it. Make the voxxa service
   plan the one the minder checks against FreeShow's project on Thursday.
