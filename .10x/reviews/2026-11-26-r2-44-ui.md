after one ten-minute session. Read: the research report, the current-state brief, the five research notes
(mostly `human_ai_training_reliability.md`), `out/spec.md` §§ 4.7, 6, 7, `out/plan.md` V10, V12, V14, V15,
ADRs 011, 017, 022, 024, 025, 031, `founder-action-items.md`, the kit checklists and prompts 05–07, and the
read-only checkout `C:\dev\r2-review` (`src/simple/*`, `stage.html`, `src/stage.js`, `src/styles/*`,
`tests/gui/*`, `scripts/loc_budget.py`, `.10x/handoff.md`). No lyrics, heard words or private files were read;
slide indices and part labels only. Claims are marked (measured), (cited), (inferred) or (opinion).

## 0. Summary

The report's step 6 ("minders, a reserved kill key, rehearsal gating, a ten-minute card") is right about the
people and wrong about its place in the queue: it is ranked last, yet it is the first thing a volunteer touches
on the first shadow Sunday, and nothing in plan.md builds it. V10 and V14 build an operator "Run" screen for a
person who sits at the PC and presses Pause; the minder is a different user doing a different job (glance, hold,
tell someone). Five things are missing from every step: the per-song "who moves it" badge, the firm/provisional
current-and-next card, a hold that is not Pause and a panic that is not Off, a one-page health check, and the
practice review that confirms or nudges instead of tapping every slide. Two existing things are wrong for the
minder: the stage window prints live heard words to the worship leader (`src/stage.js:52-55`), and the keys-only
Resume card asks a volunteer to count slides under pressure. The FreeShow move (report step 1) is, from this lens,
the single biggest UI improvement in the plan, because read-back deletes the Resume card and the
"Bring Presenter to the front" trap. Below: ten findings ranked, five wireframes, the plan changes, a draft
prompt for "Run screen and health check (minder UI)", skills and context, five founder decisions, and risks.

## 1. Findings, ranked by severity

**F1 (high). The minder has no screen.** V14's U1 subset (spec § 4.7) gives Pause/Resume, a reason line,
Sure/Not sure/Lost, an Off confirm and the Resume card (measured: `out/plan.md` V14 task 1). The report's step 6
needs, in addition: a per-song mode badge (autonomous / suggest-only / manual), the two-state current-firm and
next-provisional card (cited: USPTO 11741963 via the training note KQ3), an amber "not sure, holding" state that
names what it is holding, and hold / back one / panic as three distinct controls (cited: QLab's graded stops,
training note KQ5). None of these is in V10's mock or V14's acceptance. Fix: widen V10 (cheap, it is a mock)
and re-scope V14 (§ 4 below).

**F2 (high). The stage window shows the leader live heard words.** `stage.html:31` has `#stage-heard` and
`src/stage.js:52-55` writes every `transcription` payload into it (measured). For the worship leader this is
noise that moves every second while they sing, and if the confidence monitor is in the congregation's sight it
is text voxxa produced, not text the church's own software supplied, which is outside what ADR-011 allows
(measured: ADR-011 "text read from the church's own sources"). The leader needs one cue, not a transcript.
Fix: delete the heard-words line (frees GUI lines) and replace it with the three-word follow cue in § 2.4.

**F3 (high). Hold, panic and Off are three ideas and the plan has two buttons for them.** Spec § 4.7 has Pause
(F15/Space) and Off (with a confirm). The report wants "hold" (pause following, keep listening) and "panic"
(stop all output for the service, defined post-panic state) (cited: report § "Volunteers become minders").
A volunteer under stress must not have to choose between Pause, Off and Stop. Proposal: exactly two live
controls plus Back one: **HOLD** (toggle; voxxa keeps listening and keeps the slide; the badge turns amber) and
**STOP FOLLOWING** (red; voxxa sends nothing for the rest of the service, keeps listening and logging, every
song badge turns "You move it"; needs no confirm; recovery is Advanced > Songs again). Off stays in the mode
switch and is never a live control. Never Escape: Presenter's Escape behaviour is undocumented in the shortcuts
list (cited: WorshipTools shortcuts, landscape note KQ3) and voxxa must never share a panic key with a key it
could ever inject. Hardware: a second physical button next to HOLD on the same pedal or Stream Deck (FA-17).

**F4 (high, pilot only). On WorshipTools, the minder cannot touch voxxa's window without breaking voxxa.**
Keys go to Presenter only while Presenter is in front (measured: ADR-019 § 2, `src/simple/logic.js:11`). A
mouse click on voxxa's HOLD button gives voxxa's window the focus; the next move is refused and the light turns
amber "Bring WorshipTools Presenter to the front." The minder then has to click back into Presenter, which no
card can teach in ten minutes (inferred from the guard; not tested live). So on the pilot app every live control
must be hardware (F13–F24 through the global-shortcut plugin, ADR-024 § 6) or the HTTP route from a
Companion/Stream Deck, and the on-screen buttons are the fallback the card tells the minder to use "only if the
pedal fails, then click Presenter again". FreeShow over HTTP removes the whole problem, which is one more reason
to agree with the report's step 1.

**F5 (medium). The keys-only Resume card asks a volunteer to count slides.** "Presenter shows slide __ of __
(not counting its blank)?" with no default button (measured: spec § 4.7, S-14) is correct for safety and wrong
for a volunteer: counting slides on a projector while the band plays is the hardest task on the screen. With
FA-21 (first line on Run) answered yes, the card can ask a yes/no instead: "Is the projector showing this line?"
with the first line of the slide voxxa thinks is up, and "Yes" / "No, it is behind" / "No, it is ahead" as three
equal buttons (still no default). With FreeShow read-back the card disappears. Recommend FA-21 yes.

**F6 (medium). The amber sentence tells the minder to act.** Spec § 6: "Not sure where we are. If the screen
is behind, press Next." The report's human-factors reading says the person should hold, not fix (cited:
Bainbridge 1983; Honeycutt et al. 2020 in the report). With the parts map, "behind" is also ambiguous (it may
be a repeated chorus). Rewrite: "Not sure. Holding slide 4 of 7." and let the leader's veto (§ 2.4) or the
8-second review (ADR-031 § 4) resolve it; "press Next if behind" moves into the suggest-only badge state, where
the person is the mover anyway.

**F7 (medium). The practice review is a terminal line, not a review.** `voxxad --learn` prints per song
"V1 C V2 C B C · 6 parts · 1 unsure" and `--confirm <song_fp>` accepts all of it or nothing (measured: spec
§ 4.4, plan V12 task 3). The report's confirm-or-nudge (cited: arXiv 2306.01277 active-learning tiering, via the
report) needs the unsure part visible and nudgeable. Proposal in § 2.3: a keyboard-only review inside `--learn`
(`--review`), one song per screen, labels and counts only, Enter = yes, arrow keys to change the one unsure
label, N = not this song, within the practice row. The GUI version waits for Q14's "Learn from this rehearsal"
button, as the plan already says.

**F8 (medium). Colour carries meaning alone in two places, and the filled colours fail contrast with white
text.** Simple mode's light is a plain dot plus a sentence (fine); the stage state pill is colour plus a word
(fine); the stage BLANK pill blinks for ever (`stage.css` `@keyframes blink`) with no reduced-motion rule
(measured). Computed from `main.css`'s hex values (measured by calculation, not by tool): white on `--danger`
`#ef4444` is 3.8:1 (large text only), white on amber `#f59e0b` is 2.2:1 and white on `--success` `#22c55e` is
2.3:1 (both fail); dark `#0a0a0f` text on amber is 9.2:1 and on green 9.2:1 (pass). Rule for Run: filled
amber and green chips use dark text; red buttons use white text at 20 px bold or larger; every chip carries a
symbol (▶ following, ‖ holding, ■ stopped, ? not sure) as V14 task 2 already asks; `prefers-reduced-motion`
turns the blink into a static outline.

**F9 (low). The budgets do not fit the minder UI.** GUI row 2,859 / 3,000 at a5f7e2e (measured:
`.10x/handoff.md:130`; the brief's 2,951 is the pre-Q8 figure in ADR-017), Run row 350 planned (ADR-028). Simple
mode's switch, light, first-run and help took 450 lines (measured: handoff row table). Run screen plus health
check plus the stage strip is about 450–550 lines [est.]. Caps are never loosened (ADR-028), and a new module
gets a new recorded row: propose "health check (src/health/)" capped at 150 beside Run 350, and the stage
change inside the GUI row (removing heard words frees about 10 lines; the strip costs about 25 [est.]).

**F10 (low). The training artefacts have no home in the plan.** The report names a four-line role card and a
ten-minute session (cited: report § minders; training note KQ4 inferences) but no V-step writes them, no kit
template holds them, and the kit's `service-day-checklist.md` is a 30-item tech-lead list, not a minder card.
Proposal: `kit/templates/minder-role-card.md`, `kit/templates/minder-session.md`, `kit/prompts/09-minder-session.md`
and a skill `voxxa-minder-session`, in a small kit step (V5d, § 4).

## 2. Wireframes (ASCII; 420 px wide, the main window's size, dark theme as today)

Conventions: `[ ]` is a 44 px button; `( )` a radio in the existing mode switch; `===` a filled (firm) box;
`- - -` a dashed (provisional) box. Lower-case "voxxa" per V16. Slide text appears only as the first line and
only if FA-21 is yes; otherwise the line shows the part label and slide number.

### 2.1 Run screen during a service (Simple mode with Songs on; the "Now showing" area becomes this)

```
+------------------------------------------+
|  voxxa                                   |
|  ( Off )  (* Songs *)  ( Songs+Scripture)|
|                                          |
|  ● Following "It Is Well", slide 4 of 8. |   <- the existing light and sentence, unchanged
|                                          |
|  Song 2 of 5 · It Is Well                |
|  [▶ voxxa moves it]                      |   <- per-song badge: ▶ voxxa moves it / ? voxxa suggests / ✋ You move it
|                                          |
|  ================ NOW ================   |
|  = C · slide 4 of 8                   =  |   <- firm: part label, slide number; first line if FA-21 yes
|  = <first line of slide 4>            =  |
|  ======================================  |
|  - - - - - - - - NEXT - - - - - - - - -  |
|  . V3 · slide 5 of 8 · likely         .  |   <- provisional: grey, dashed, the word "likely"
|  . <first line of slide 5>            .  |      or "?" when the parts map has two candidates
|  - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -   |
|  Last move: heard the words. 0.3 s ago.  |   <- the reason line from V14, unchanged
|                                          |
|  [ ‖ HOLD ]  [ ◀ Back one ]  [ Next ▶ ]  |   <- 44 px; HOLD is a toggle; F15 / F14 / F13
|  [ ■ STOP FOLLOWING (rest of service) ]  |   <- red, white 20 px bold; F20; no confirm
|                                          |
|  [ Call for help ]           Advanced    |
+------------------------------------------+
```

The amber "not sure, holding" state changes three things and nothing else: the light turns amber with
"Not sure. Holding slide 4 of 8."; the badge keeps its word but takes the amber chip; the NEXT box shows
"?" with no label. No countdown, no percentage (cited: no live-captioning or teleprompter system exposes a
numeric confidence, training note KQ3). After a manual Next or Back the badge changes to "✋ You move it" for
the rest of that song and the sentence says "You moved it. voxxa suggests until the next song." (report:
a manual press demotes the song; Amershi G18 "notify about changes"). HOLD: light grey-amber, sentence
"Holding slide 4 of 8. Press HOLD again to let voxxa move." STOP FOLLOWING: red light, "Stopped. You move
every slide today. voxxa still listens and keeps its log."

Deliberately not shown: heard words, the heard-token cache, numeric certainty, onset fires, the pace clock,
the plan order, the full slide text (ADR-011: the presenter already shows the words), key lists, log paths.

```
+------------------------------------------+
|  Ready for today                          |   <- one of: "Ready for today" (green) /
|  ● Ready, with one note.                  |      "Ready, with a note" (amber) / "Not ready" (red)
|                                          |
|  ●  Sound       Hearing the singers' mic. |
|                 Level good, line clean.   |
|  ●  Slides app  FreeShow is open and      |
|                 answering.                |
|  ◐  Songs       5 songs loaded for today. |
|                 1 not rehearsed: voxxa    |
|                 suggests on it, you move. |
|  ●  Practice    Learned yesterday (Sat).  |
|  ○  HOLD test   Press HOLD once now.      |   <- turns ● "HOLD works." when the pedal press arrives
|  ●  Log         voxxa can write its log.  |
|                                          |
|  Today: voxxa moves 4 songs, suggests 1. |
|                                          |
|  [ Start Songs ]        [ Check again ]  |   <- Start is enabled on green and amber; red disables
|  Tell the tech lead if a line is red.    |      only the Slides-app red (nothing can move)

```

The six rows are the report's six checks (report § minders: level and hum, app reachable, setlist with
rehearsal status, learned state matches the last rehearsal, kill-switch test press, log writable). Every
line is colour plus a symbol (● ok, ◐ note, ○ not yet, ✕ problem) plus a sentence. Exact words for each
amber and red line, written to the `statusLight` style (`src/simple/logic.js`): short, no jargon, the next
action in the second sentence.

| Row | Amber (a note) | Red (not ready) |
|---|---|---|
| Sound | "The mic is quiet. Ask the sound desk to turn up voxxa's feed." / "There is a hum on the line. voxxa may be late on soft starts." | "Can't hear the mic. Check the cable, then press Check again." |
| Slides app | keys-only: "Presenter is open. Keep it in front all service." | "FreeShow is not answering. Open it, then press Check again." / "Presenter runs as administrator; voxxa's keys can't reach it." (existing sentence) |
| Songs | "2 of 5 not rehearsed: voxxa suggests on them, you move them." / "This looks like last week's plan. Check the date in Advanced." | "No songs loaded. Load today's plan in Advanced first." |
| Practice | "Learned 3 weeks ago. Words changed on 1 song: voxxa suggests on it." | (never red: Sunday is never blocked, ADR-026 spirit) |
| HOLD test | "HOLD not pressed yet. Press it once so we know it works." | "HOLD did not arrive. Use the button on screen today and tell the tech lead." |
| Log | (none) | "voxxa can't save its log (disk full?). It will still follow. Tell the tech lead." |

"Shadow" days show a seventh line at the top: "Shadow today: voxxa listens and logs, moves nothing." The
level and hum thresholds come from the kit's § 8 check (peaks about −20 to −6 dBFS; quiet at least 30 dB
below, est.) and are never shown as numbers to the minder; the tech lead sees them in Advanced.

### 2.3 Practice-session review (keyboard only; `voxxad --learn … --review`; labels and counts only)

```
Rehearsal 2026-12-06-am · 5 songs · 3 to look at
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Song 2 of 5 · It Is Well                              [2 / 5]

  Heard order:   V1   C   V2   C   V3?  C   V4   C
                                  ^^^
  One part unsure (3.7 s of words fit V3 and B about the same).

  Enter   Yes, that is what we sang           -> confirms this song
  ← →     Change the unsure part  (V3 ◂ ▸ B)  -> then Enter
  N       No, we sang it differently          -> left unconfirmed, never used
  S       Skip for now                        -> stays proposed
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Confirmed 1 · unconfirmed 0 · skipped 0 · not rehearsed 1 (song 5)
```

Rules: only songs with an unsure part or an order different from the plan are shown first; a song whose
heard order equals the plan with no unsure part shows one line "Song 1 · matches the plan · Enter to confirm"
(auto-accept confident, offer a label for intermediate, hand-label hard: the tiering in arXiv 2306.01277 via
the report). Nothing prints a word. The last screen asks once: "Use what voxxa learned today on Sunday?
[Enter] yes / [N] no", which is the `--confirm` of every song confirmed above. The founder's "accept a song's
learned state" is therefore one Enter per song and one Enter at the end; tapping every slide (`vocal_test.py`
tap mode, 524 ms reaction lag) is kept only for making truth files, not for learning.

### 2.4 Stage display for the worship leader (voxxa's stage window as a strip, or a FreeShow stage layout)

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
|  ▶ voxxa following            It Is Well · C · 4 of 8        |   <- one strip, 48 px high, bottom of the monitor
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
   (FreeShow's own stage layout shows the current and next slide text above it, as today)
```

Three states only, each colour + symbol + two words: green "▶ voxxa following", amber "‖ voxxa holding",
grey "✋ You're driving" (after STOP FOLLOWING or a manual move). No heard words (F2), no percentage, no
animation; a state change fades over 300 ms, and not at all under reduced motion. The veto reachable from the
stage is one hardware button on the music stand or the leader's pedal: HOLD (F15) over Companion to voxxa's
HTTP `POST /api/v1/pause` (spec § 4.6); a second press resumes. Back one (F14) is offered to leaders who ask for
it, not by default (opinion: a leader mid-song should have one button). Whether FreeShow lets an outside program
push text into a stage layout is not stated in its docs (cited: landscape note KQ2, "The page does not say an
external program can push text to stage"), so I don't know whether the strip can live inside FreeShow's stage;
voxxa's own stage window, positioned by the operator once, is the safe assumption until W1's contract test.

```

  1. Open voxxa. Choose Songs. Read the top line: green or amber = go; red = tell the tech lead.
```

### 2.6 The ten-minute session (kit template; run by the founder first, then by a veteran)

| Minute | What happens | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Watch voxxa follow one rehearsed song from a recording in shadow (`live.py --dry-run --wav`, or the GUI mock). The trainer points at the badge and the NOW / NEXT boxes. | Trainee says what ▶, ?, ✋ mean |

## 3. Verdict on the report's ranked path

**Agree with step 1 (FreeShow jump driver with read-back) as rank 1**, for a UI reason the report does not
state: read-back deletes the two worst screens in the plan, the keys-only Resume card (F5) and the "Bring
Presenter to the front" trap (F4), and makes the "position uncertain" state (ADR-025 § 2) a thing voxxa detects
instead of a question it asks a volunteer (inferred from ADR-025 and the FreeShow `get_output` getters cited in
the report).

**Change the order of step 6.** Its artefacts are not last; they are needed by the first shadow Sunday a
volunteer attends. Split it: the health check, the hold and stop controls, the badge and the role card move up
to land with the first live-config work (plan weeks 5–8, V10 → V14), before any shadow Sunday with a minder;
only the four-Sunday acceptance stays at the end. Steps 2, 4 and 5 are invisible to the minder except through
the badge and the practice review; step 3 (aligner) is invisible entirely and can stay where it is.

**Reject one detail of step 6:** "a reserved kill key" modelled on QLab's Escape. On the pilot app, no key voxxa
could inject may double as its panic, and Escape's effect in Presenter is undocumented (F3). The pattern that
transfers is "unremappable and graded", not "Escape".

**Agree with the two-state convention and the hold-on-uncertainty default** (cited: USPTO 11741963, PromptSmart,
via the report and training note KQ3), with the caveat that the "next" box earns trust only when it is right;
show "likely" only with a confirmed practice run for that song, and "?" otherwise (opinion, from Zhang et al.
2020's finding that confidence display calibrates trust only when the human can add knowledge).

## 8. Risks the report missed

1. **The focus trap on the pilot app (F4).** Any mouse touch on voxxa's window while Presenter must be in front
   stops voxxa's keys. The card must say it; the hardware button must exist before the first minder Sunday
   (inferred from ADR-019 § 2; not tested live).
2. **Trust erosion from a wrong "likely".** A NEXT box that is wrong twice teaches the leader and the minder to
   ignore it (cited: Zhang et al. 2020 via the report). Show "likely" only with a confirmed practice run.
3. **Alarm fatigue from amber flicker.** Sure/Not sure may flip every second near the 0.80/0.50 thresholds
   (spec V14 task 1). Add hysteresis (a state must hold for 2 windows) and keep the change-only `put` writes,
   or the aria-live sentence chatters to a screen-reader user (inferred).
4. **The stage window on a visible display.** ADR-011's consequences already warn; a strip is still text. Keep
   the Q2 check in V14 and add "voxxa's windows are not on the projector or stream" to the role card's step 1.
5. **Rotation and Windows accounts.** `localStorage` keys (`voxxa.firstLaunchDone`, `voxxa.presenterForm`) are
   per Windows user (measured: ADR-017 § 4). If minders log in with their own accounts, each sees the first-run
   guide and an unconnected presenter. The role card assumes one booth account; the health check's slides-app
   red catches the rest.
6. **"Two volunteers pass the card" has no test.** The report's acceptance needs the § 2.6 "done when" lines or
   it cannot be checked; V5d supplies them.
7. **Operator notes versus the score.** Prompt 07 already says notes are context, never the score; the Run
   screen should log HOLD, STOP and manual moves with times so the review does not depend on memory (the
   report's "overrides and hold time" row, not yet logged).
8. **I don't know** whether FreeShow's stage layout can show an outside cue (landscape KQ2), what Escape does in
   Presenter, whether Tauri can detect a duplicated display (spec § 11), or how many lines the Run card really
   costs; the mock in V10 answers the last one before V14 commits to a row.
