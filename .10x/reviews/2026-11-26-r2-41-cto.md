## 1. Findings, ranked by severity

**F1 (high). The shipped FreeShow driver cannot jump.** `freeshow.rs:48-51` sends `{ "action": a, "data": {...} }`, so
`goto_slide` sends `{ "action": "index_select_slide", "data": { "index": n } }` (measured). FreeShow's REST handler
does `let data = req.body || {}` and forwards the whole object; fields sit beside `action`, never under `data`;
non-getters answer 204 (cited: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChurchApps/FreeShow/main/src/electron/utils/api.ts).
W1 is therefore settled from public source: the index never arrives (inferred). The report says "may be ignoring". The
fix is one line in a 170/400-line driver (presenters row 626 free, measured), but nothing about FreeShow has ever run
live, so the fix must land with the Q13-style contract fixture or we ship the same mistake twice.

**F2 (high). Two gates confused.** ADR-022 § 3 and FA-05: the follower default flips after **2** consecutive shadow
Sundays with a volunteer present. The report's "four consecutive shadow Sundays ... without a volunteer" is a later,
different gate (no minder at the PC). Keep both as two FA items (FA-52), or the default flip slips a month for nothing.

**F3 (medium). "Switch the live output one rehearsed song at a time" is not operable.** One projector source, two apps
and the band's stage display mid-service is a worse Sunday than a wrong slide (opinion). Replace with a "dark
FreeShow" stage: voxxa drives FreeShow with no output assigned, weekdays then Sundays, while Presenter stays live; the
output switches once, whole-service, with a volunteer at FreeShow's keys and voxxa suggest-only (§ 2.3).

**F4 (medium). Model licences.** The report calls torchaudio MMS_FA "BSD"; torchaudio's code is BSD, Meta's MMS
weights are CC-BY-NC 4.0 (inferred from the MMS release; **verify**). Park et al.'s aligner code is CC-BY-NC 4.0 (cited
in the alignment note, line 24). LyricsAlignment-MTL's code is MIT; its checkpoints state no licence and were trained
on DALI v2 (cited: https://github.com/jhuang448/LyricsAlignment-MTL). ADR-005 excludes non-commercial model licences
and `deny.toml` allows no NC licence (measured). CC-NC is lab-only, never product or kit (FA-50).

**F5 (medium). VRAM is unmeasured with FreeShow open.** Small sits at 814 of 2048 MB with Presenter open (measured);
FreeShow is Electron with a GPU-composited output window on the GTX 960 that also drives the projector. The spike
measures it before any Sunday with both apps. An aligner beside Whisper on this GPU gets no budget row until measured.

**F6 (medium). The input chain has no row.** Report step 4 (channel select, high-pass, gain, energy gate, level and hum
check) lands in `listen.rs`, i.e. the "wiring" row FA-10 gives exactly 150 lines, shared with V8's dispatch fixes and
V13's hotkeys and routes; "everything else" has 1 line left (measured, handoff row table). ADR-028: a new module gets a
new recorded row (FA-54), decided at V3b's port plan.

**F7 (low). Bitfocus Companion as the volunteer surface** adds a third app to the booth and contradicts ADR-006's
reasoning. voxxa already has Pause (F15, Space, HTTP) and Off (ADR-024 § 6). Hold = Pause, panic = Off; write the card,
build nothing.

**F8 (low). Metric renaming.** Document the mir_eval mapping in `fixtures/README.md`; keep voxxa-eval's 8 names and
`baseline.json` (ADR-015). **F9 (low).** Per-leader resume priors depend on V12; hand-set handicaps until then.

**F10 (info). FreeShow health is good.** ChurchApps organisation, 1.4k stars, 402 forks (cited in the landscape note,
line 81); betas 1.6.8-beta.1 to beta.6 dated 28 Oct to 26 Nov, 19 pages of releases (cited:
https://github.com/ChurchApps/FreeShow/releases). Date of the last stable release: I don't know; the spike records it.
Weekly betas plus an Electron auto-updater are a Sunday risk: stable only, pinned (FA-51).

### 2.2 Migration cost against value (pilot church)

Costs (inferred): the library re-import is "import the church's `songs\*.txt` into FreeShow", because ADR-026/FA-14 (a)
already make `.txt` + `songs.csv` the single source and V5 step 9 keeps section labels; unknown: FreeShow's import
formats and whether labels become groups (the spike counts). Planning Center: voxxa imports PCO plans today
(`importers/planning_center/`, measured) and FreeShow syncs services a week ahead (cited): less double entry, not more.
Stage display: Presenter's is a paid tier, FreeShow's is free; the band sees a new screen on switch day, so the worship
leader is a stakeholder. Volunteers: Q11 stays on Presenter; retraining is one ten-minute card after the switch
decision. Founder time ≈ 6–8 h over six weeks [est.]. Cash $0; FA-38 (b) is needed either way.

Value: jumps in one call, so ADR-031's 74 of 77 (measured) can be shown rather than logged; read-back retires the
ADR-025 freeze and the front-window guard for jumps; **a free truth trace for shadow Sundays**: polling `get_output`
while a volunteer drives FreeShow gives a time-stamped record of the slide shown, which V15's scoring lacks today
(ADR-019: voxxa is blind on Presenter; ≈ 2 h of tapping per Sunday); remote observation over FreeShow's LAN stage and
remote pages instead of RDP (cited in the landscape note, line 92); and a lower church-side bus factor: a church left
with FreeShow and a `.txt` library sits on a maintained free app, not on a key-pressing rig. Verdict: value exceeds
cost once the spike confirms groups, VRAM and import (opinion).

## 5. The multi-church kit

FreeShow **helps** church #2: free, Windows-native, PCO sync, and its profile is already a kit template
(`out/kit/templates/bindings/freeshow.json`, measured). The app-agnostic layer is ADR-024's binding profiles with the
`via`/`hint` fallback; the follower never knows the app. What must stay true: **two tiers stated in the kit and on the
Run screen** (FA-47): Tier A "jump and read-back" apps (FreeShow, ProPresenter, Holyrics, OpenLP partly) are eligible
for autonomy; Tier B keys-only apps (Presenter, universal) are assisted: in-plan steps, suggestion cards, a minder at
the keys. That keeps Presenter a supported fallback with a truthful promise. **One song source**: `.txt` + `songs.csv`
feed voxxa, Presenter and FreeShow, and ADR-024's arrangement labels (`v1 c v2 ...`) must match the FreeShow group names
the import produces, or `id_select_group` has no target (the spike checks with the PD hymns). Kit additions are listed
in § "Skills and context files". Cost to the kit: two paths to validate on the fake church in V15; acceptable, because
the alternative is a kit that can never promise autonomy (opinion).

## 6. Strategic risks

1. **GPL FreeShow dependency**: none as a licence matter; HTTP to a separate process, no linking (inferred; ADR-005
   already lists FreeShow as a GPL peer). Upstream contributions (a name-based group selector) are GPL-to-GPL, easy.
2. **FreeShow health**: active (F10); the risk is weekly betas and auto-update changing behaviour mid-week. Pin, stable
   only, version in `church-profile.json`, contract fixtures as the regression net (FA-51).
3. **2 GB VRAM, one GPU for the projector**: unmeasured (F5); fallback is CPU at V3's settings or closing Presenter after
   the switch. No aligner until measured.
4. **Single-founder bus factor**: a kit page "run without voxxa" (FreeShow or Presenter by hand), `.10x/` as the
   memory, a 170-line driver, DCO sign-offs. FreeShow lowers the church-side risk (§ 2.2).
5. **Model licences** (F4, FA-50): Whisper and whisper.cpp are MIT; CC-NC stays in the founder's lab.
6. **Read-back privacy**: `get_output` can carry slide text and media paths; the binding note keeps ids only (measured).
   Make it a named test; never log the body (ADR-011, ADR-013).
7. **Two libraries drift** during the parallel period; `.txt` + `songcheck` is the control; a service-day row "FreeShow
   library row count = `songs.csv`".
8. **Social risk**: the band and the worship leader change screens on switch day; FA-48 needs their yes.

1. The shipped driver cannot jump (F1); W1 is a fact, not a maybe.
2. Read-back as a free truth source for shadow Sundays; not using it keeps ≈ 2 h of tapping per Sunday.
3. One projector, two apps: "one song at a time" is unworkable (F3).
4. FreeShow's weekly betas and auto-update as a Sunday risk (FA-51).
5. Model-weight licences versus ADR-005 (F4, FA-50).
6. The input chain has no budget row (F6).
7. Default-flip gate confused with the no-minder gate (F2).
8. Companion as a third app for volunteers (F7).
9. The band and the worship leader as stakeholders of the switch, not only the slide volunteer.
10. Two libraries drifting during the parallel period; `.txt` + `songcheck` is the control.
11. `get_output` carries slide text and media paths; an ids-only test is a must (ADR-011).
12. Holyrics as a fallback has its own bus factor (closed-source freeware, no PCO found); build nothing speculatively.
