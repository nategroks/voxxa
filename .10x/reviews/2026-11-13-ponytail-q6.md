- A lane that commits nothing gives the comparator nothing to choose. The ponytail lane's result was work in progress that also changed behaviour outside the remit.

### 10.2 The re-run's design
**Scope:** the smallest change that replaces only the conductor's next-slide check (`maybe_advance_within_current`) with ADR-009's tracker. Song detection, `MachineState`, blanking, `manual_step`, `jump_to_song` and the public API are not touched.
- **Code:** one new file, `src-tauri/src/aligner/evidence/mod.rs`, with `Section { kind, start }` and `Tracker { at, evidence }`. The conductor calls `Tracker::hop` from `maybe_advance_within_current`. On a move it does the same bookkeeping as before: clear the buffer, set the last-advance time, enter `Singing` and emit the goto.
- **Score:** the last 12 recognised word tokens. The current slide is scored against its whole text. Any other slide is scored against the current slide's last 12 tokens followed by that slide's first 24, which are the words the room sings just after a move. After a blank, a slide is scored against its whole text. The score is the token LCS divided by the number of recent tokens.
- **Evidence:** `e = decay·e + (1 − decay)·prior·score`. Priors: stay 1.0, next slide 1.0, a chorus or refrain start 1.0, any other section start 0.95 (and every slide of an unlabelled song), anything else 0.5.
- **Decision:** move to the best slide when it is not the current one, its evidence is at least 0.7, it leads every other slide by 0.1 (identical copies of it excepted), and the current slide has been up for 2,000 ms.
- **Operator:** no hook in `manual_step`. The tracker resets when the conductor's position differs from the one its evidence was gathered at.
- **Data and config:** setlist v1 gains optional `sections: [{kind, start}]`, skipped when empty, so fingerprints and logs do not change. `SmartConfig` gets a container-level `#[serde(default)]`. `slide_advance_debounce_ms` and `advance_margin` were deleted.
- **Tuning:** a simulator over the committed recogniser streams (it printed indices and times only). The results stay the same over a wide range of every value. Two choices came from the fixtures. Scoring other slides against their whole text gave 2 false advances on the refrain fixture, because the refrain shares a line with verse 1. A margin over the current slide only gave a false jump to verse 4 in `prayer-between-songs`, because that hymn's verses share their opening words.
- **Not built:** next-song and other-song candidates, adapter `current()` re-sync, importers that write `sections`, any GUI change. The fixture setlists were left unlabelled, so the eval gains come from the words alone.
- The lane wrote a proposed ADR-016 text into its report, not into `.10x`. It was not used, because the comparator kept `q6/base`, whose ADR-016 already stands.

**Lessons.**
- A lane lost to the content filter costs a full re-run. With the content rule in every prompt, the re-run, its review, the comparator, the finisher and the verifiers all finished without a stop.
- Two independent designs reached the same core (a 12-token window, an LCS score, evidence with decay) and the same gains inside a song. Base's lead came from the Q5-04 change, which the re-run left out of scope. A strict minimum can lose on an eval that also measures song starts.
- Gate 5 turned red between 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-18 with no dependency change, because the advisory database changed. A green gate is only true on the day it ran (R13).
- An independent lane's tests are cheap to graft and check the base from outside: the ported trace confirmed that base's Q5-04 change leaves the pre-Q6 blanking behaviour alone.
