## 1. The task, in short
Run `voxxa-eval` on every fixture. If false advances ≤ 1 per 10-song set and p95 latency ≤ 2.5 s, write "ADR-009 deferred" and stop. Otherwise build ADR-009's minimum: `sections[].kind` in setlist v1, repeat-aware transitions with priors from `SmartConfig`, word-token scoring on the last 12 recognised words, an evidence accumulator with decay and `min_dwell_ms`, keeping `MachineState`, blanking and `manual_step` untouched. Scripted tests: repeated chorus, bridge jump, dropped words, operator override. Record before/after. Exit: a decision file and numbers either way; the eval not worse on any fixture.

User decisions (binding, 2026-11-13): ADR-016 is the number; 2-3 synthetic TTS fixtures of public-domain hymns first; one new budget row "conductor evidence (ADR-009 minimum)" capped at 500, with the conductor row kept at 1,200; `baseline.json` tightened, never loosened; no GUI change beyond what labels strictly need; keep the public API, `MachineState`, blanking and `manual_step`; no sockets.

**10x lane, in short.** Optional `sections[].kind` per song (old setlists serialise byte for byte). Priors from `SmartConfig.evidence`: stay 1.0, next 0.9 (after a chorus, the verse after the one that led in), chorus start 0.8, section start 0.6, other 0.4, and 0 for the slide an operator just left. Score: the share of the newest words that extend an LCS alignment of the last 12 words with a slide, or that match a slide's leading words when it is being entered. Accumulator `E = 0.3·E + 0.7·prior·s`; move at `E ≥ 0.35`, a 0.05 lead and 1,500 ms of dwell. Q5-04 pulled in: while blank, song detection reads only the newest transcript. ## 9. Lessons for the next step
- A per-fixture latency gate reads a recall gain as a regression when the fixture's old hits were few. ADR-016 predicted it in its risks before any code. Q7, and any later threshold rule, should say how a row is judged when recall rises.
- Building an old commit in the shared `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` overwrites the debug binaries of the head. Verifier A avoided this by building the export under a target-triple subfolder; the finisher rebuilt the head before measuring.
- A lane that commits nothing gives the comparator nothing to choose. The ponytail lane's result was work in progress that also changed behaviour outside the remit.
