## Q4-01 [medium, performance, tooling] Release builds compile whisper.cpp without optimisation on Windows
**What.** A release `voxxad` decodes a 5 s window in 6.4-8.6 s with Whisper Tiny; a debug build takes 1.8-1.9 s on the same PC. The shipped GUI is a release build. (Was Q4-a.)
**Where.** `src-tauri/Cargo.toml:96-100` (`[profile.release]`, `opt-level = "s"`); the `whisper-rs-sys` 0.11 build script; `.github/workflows/build.yml` (release builds).
**Why.** `whisper-rs-sys` always asks cmake for the `Release` config. On MSVC the `cmake` crate overwrites `CMAKE_C_FLAGS_RELEASE` and `CMAKE_CXX_FLAGS_RELEASE` with the `cc` flags minus every `/O` flag, so whisper.cpp is built unoptimised. Debug builds escape it because the crate sets `RelWithDebInfo` there. In the shipped app a hop would take about 6.5 s on the 4-core reference PC, slower than the old 5 s batch. `[profile.release.package.whisper-rs-sys] opt-level = 3` was measured and gives no gain.
**Suggested fix.** Set `CMAKE_C_FLAGS_RELEASE` and `CMAKE_CXX_FLAGS_RELEASE` to `-nologo -MD -Brepro -W0 -O2 -Ob2 -DNDEBUG` for Windows release builds in CI and packaging (`whisper-rs-sys` forwards `CMAKE_*` variables; measured 1.79-1.94 s per hop). Check whether Linux and macOS release builds have the same problem. Add a release-build timing line to the QA recipe.

## Q4-02 [medium, performance] A hop costs more than 1 s on the reference PC, so live hypotheses come every 2-5 s
**What.** Even in an optimised build, one decode of a 5 s window takes about 1.8 s on the 4-core PC. With `--realtime`, `--device` and in the GUI the gate skips hops, so a window is decoded every 2-5 s, not every second. Under heavy load (decodes of 10-20 s) verifier A saw the bounded `voxxad` feeder (64 chunks, 6.4 s) block and audio slip, and that `--realtime` run had no blank before the stop. (Was Q4-b.)
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/transcription/mod.rs:174-203` (`words`: `create_state` per decode; the window is padded to Whisper's 30 s); `src-tauri/src/listen.rs` (decode on the loop thread); `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxad/main.rs:248` (`sync_channel(64)`).
**Why.** Work stays bounded (ADR-014 Decision 5), but detection latency in the room is up to one decode plus one hop, and ticks and VAD still stall during a decode (Q3-06, #50).
**Suggested fix.** Measure each separately with `voxxad --realtime --shadow`: `params.set_audio_ctx` sized for 5 s of audio (about 6 times less encoder work; check accuracy with `voxxa-eval`), one reused `WhisperState` instead of `create_state` per decode (about 180 MB allocated per hop), and then decoding on a worker thread (#50). Record real-time factor and tick lag.

## Q4-03 [medium] Blank timing is unmeasured: lyrics stay up for the first 5-7 s of a prayer
**What.** A prayer that follows a song without a long silence blanks only once 5 s have passed since its first unmatched transcript (`unrecognized_speech_to_blank_secs`), through the transcript rule or the tick rule. Verifier A saw the last lyric slide stay up for about 6.7 s of an 8.1 s prayer in 3 of 4 runs. The other side of the same rule: 5 s of misheard song in the middle of a verse blanks the lyrics.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:320-356` (`tick`, the tick blank rule from b2b8d0b) and `:450-470` (`on_transcript`, the 5 s rule from 2bdd2b9); `SmartConfig` defaults at `:73-74`; ADR-014 Risks.
**Why.** ADR-011 forbids projecting lyrics where they do not belong; a prayer with the previous verse still on screen is visible to the room. A false mid-song blank is a bad blank, never a wrong slide. Both sides are tuned on synthetic speech only.
**Suggested fix.** Q5's `voxxa-eval` reports blank latency against the operator's truth; Q6 tunes `unrecognized_speech_to_blank_secs` and `silence_to_blank_secs` with those numbers. Add a fixture with a prayer straight after a song (no pause).

## Q4-04 [medium] Advances often come late in the new verse
**What.** With the stitcher, the conductor hears each word once, but its 40-word buffer still holds the previous verse when the new one starts. In the scripted test at 2 words a second, slide 1 came 9.5 s into a 12 s verse. In the exit demo goto Amazing Grace/1 came at 25.0 s for a verse sung from 16.0 s. (Part of the old Q4-e.)
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:370-376` (the 40-word buffer, `max_buffer_words`) and `maybe_advance_within_current` (`:551`); `src-tauri/src/transcription/recognizer.rs:61` (`MIN_WORDS = 4`, the stitcher's hold).
**Why.** Q6's budget is p95 latency at most 2.5 s. Late advances leave the old verse up while the room sings the new one.
**Suggested fix.** Measure latency per slide with `voxxa-eval` (Q5). Q6 decides: score the last 12 recognised words (ADR-009's minimum), or shrink the buffer after an advance. The conductor row has 12 lines left (1,188/1,200), so Q6 needs room or a budget decision.

## Q4-05 [low, performance] Conductor scoring time grows with the setlist and now runs every 1-3 s
**What.** `on_transcript` scores every song on each stitched transcript. The ponytail review measured 174 ms with 3 songs, 519 ms with 10 and 2,642 ms with 50 (opt-level 3); the finisher measured about 70 ms per song in release. Transcripts now come every 1-3 s of speech, not every 5 s.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/aligner/mod.rs:138` (`MAX_SETLIST_SONGS = 50`) and `score_all_songs`.
**Why.** The 50-song cap was justified as "well under the 5 s it takes to hear the next one". The bound now rests on the lag gate, which counts scoring time and skips hops; a long setlist therefore means fewer decodes, not a growing backlog.
**Suggested fix.** Profile scoring in release; cache each song's lowercase joined text (today `matches_song` rebuilds it per call). Revisit the cap with numbers.

## Q4-06 [low] Stitcher edge cases: a repeat right after a short pause can be dropped
**What.** The stitcher keeps the words heard across a pause shorter than a window (5 s) and lines new windows up against them by text. A phrase that repeats the words just before the pause (a repeated line, an echoed chorus) can line up with its first time and be dropped. The same limit applies within continuous speech. Separately, fewer than 4 held words at a pause are dropped, not handed to the conductor; they reach only the session log's `window` line.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/transcription/recognizer.rs:65-134` (`Stitcher`, `pause`, `overlap`); `src-tauri/src/listen.rs:164` (`stitcher.pause(...)`).
**Why.** A dropped repeat can delay an advance on a repeated-chorus arrangement. It never causes a wrong slide.
**Suggested fix.** Measure with `voxxa-eval` on a fixture with a repeated line after a short pause. If it matters, use word times as more than a tie-break once they are reliable (Q4-13), or a LocalAgreement-style commit (ADR-014 alternatives).

## Q4-07 [low, tests] Rolling's word-time clamp is checked only by the local real-model test
**What.** Removing the monotonic clamp in Rolling's word-time scan passes every CI test; only the real-model test, which needs `ggml-tiny.bin`, fails (verifier B, mutation M3b).
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/transcription/recognizer.rs:242` (`*last = t.max(*last).min(end - start)`); `src-tauri/tests/recognizer.rs`.
**Why.** CI has no model, so a regression there would reach `main` unnoticed. Word times feed the stitcher's tie-break.
**Suggested fix.** Add a fake `Decode` case that returns out-of-order and out-of-window token times, and assert the clamped stream times.

## Q4-08 [low, tests] The GUI glue closures in `start_listening_with_state` have no test
**What.** `gui_event` and `gui_sink` are pure and tested (Q3-02, #46). The two closures that connect them to Tauri are not: replacing the `send` closure's `operator` with `operator || true` (N8c') or ignoring `name` in the `emit` closure (N8g) passes every test. Only a plain `true` is caught, by clippy's unused-variable lint. (Was Q4-g.)
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/commands/mod.rs:241-246`.
**Why.** `operator = true` changes `dispatch::drive`'s check on a song change, so the blank on an unconfirmed song change could be skipped. Testing the closures needs a real `AppHandle`.
**Suggested fix.** Drop the `operator` parameter from `send`, so `gui_sink` owns the `false` and the closure cannot change it. Pass `name` through a typed helper the tests already cover.

## Q4-09 [low, tests, tooling] The "no model" path cannot be checked on a Windows machine that has the model
**What.** CI has no Whisper model, so the real-model test must skip there. A verifier tried to check that locally by pointing `APPDATA` at an empty folder; it had no effect, because the `dirs` crate reads the folder through `SHGetKnownFolderPath`, so the test still found the model.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/transcription/mod.rs:71` (`dirs::data_dir()`); `src-tauri/tests/recognizer.rs:262` (the early return); `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxad/main.rs:311`.
**Why.** The skip path is checked only by reading the code. Q3's records also mention runs with `APPDATA` pointed elsewhere; no Q3 test used a model, so nothing was missed then, but the method does not work on Windows.
**Suggested fix.** Let the tests take the model folder from an environment variable (for example `VOXXA_MODEL_DIR`) through the existing `TranscriptionEngine::with_model_dir`, and add the no-model run to the local QA recipe.

## Q4-10 [low, performance, tests] Debug test time grew again
**What.** `voxxad_events` (31 tests) takes about 150-210 s in a debug build, mostly the conductor's scoring once per hop and the VAD over minutes of audio. The real-model test takes 17-37 s alone and up to 371 s under load. (Was Q4-f and Q4-i.)
**Where.** `src-tauri/tests/voxxad_events.rs`, `src-tauri/tests/recognizer.rs`; `src-tauri/Cargo.toml` profiles.
**Why.** CI runs the suite on three operating systems; local runs are slow for every agent. Adds to Q3-13 (#57).
**Suggested fix.** As in #57: profile first; try `[profile.dev.package."*"] opt-level = 2` or opt-level for the crate's own test profile; use smaller setlists where a test does not need six songs. Consider filing this as a comment on #57 instead of a new issue.

## Q4-11 [low, performance] `--bias` leaks memory and its effect is unmeasured
**What.** `whisper-rs` 0.13's `set_initial_prompt` leaks its C string on every call, about 200 bytes per hop, while `bias_enabled` is on (only `voxxad --bias` today). ADR-004's E6 risk (Whisper echoing the prompt as a hallucination) has not been measured. (Was Q4-d.)
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/transcription/mod.rs:202-203` (the `shortcut:` comment and the call).
**Why.** A whole service with biasing would leak a few MB. Turning biasing on by default needs E6 numbers.
**Suggested fix.** Re-check when `whisper-rs` is bumped. Run `voxxa-eval` with and without `--bias` on the fixtures before any default change (ADR-014 review trigger).

## Q4-12 [low, performance] The VAD still collects every utterance's audio, which nothing reads
**What.** `VadEngine` appends every speech window to `speech_buffer` and hands the utterance back when it ends. Since Q4 the loop uses only the speech flag; the audio goes to the recogniser directly.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/vad/mod.rs:44`, `:111-121`, `:174`.
**Why.** A long unbroken utterance (a sermon read without pauses) holds 64 KB of samples per second until it ends. It is freed at each pause, so it is bounded but wasted.
**Suggested fix.** Drop `speech_buffer` and the completed-utterance return, or make them optional; check the GUI and `voxxad` callers.

## Q4-13 [low, adr] Whisper Tiny's word times are rough and are not logged
**What.** Whisper Tiny times the first word of a window at the window start (`0.13, 0.12, 0.12, 0.12, 1.04, 2.03, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0` in the real-model test), and late tokens can land past the window, which Rolling clamps. Word times are not in the session log (ADR-014 Decision 4), so `voxxa-eval` can only time actions and lines, not words.
**Where.** `src-tauri/src/transcription/recognizer.rs` (`words_from_tokens`, the clamp at `:242`); `src-tauri/src/bin/voxxad/events.rs` (the `window` and `hypothesis` lines).
**Why.** A time cutoff in the stitcher failed for this reason (c288ef7, replaced by 065b83f). Any later feature that relies on word timing (word-level latency in the eval, a smarter stitcher) needs better times.
**Suggested fix.** Try whisper.cpp's DTW token timestamps or segment-level times. If the eval needs them, add word times to the `window` line as an optional field (additive in v1), and record it as an ADR-014 note.
