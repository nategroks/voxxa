# 43 — SRE review: will it stay up on a Sunday morning with nobody at the slide PC, and how do we know

Reviewer: Site Reliability Engineer lens · Date 2026-11-26 · Under review: `reports/Voxxa autonomous slide following research.md`, read against the current-state brief, `out/{spec,plan,process}.md`, ADRs 021–031, `kit/`, the read-only checkout `C:\dev\r2-review` (a5f7e2e) and `lab/live.py`. Claims are marked (measured), (cited), (inferred) or (opinion). Private song = P1; the church and the booth PC are not named.

## 0. Summary

The report's minder model is the right shape and most of it is already written down somewhere in ADR-022, ADR-024, ADR-025 and the kit. What is missing is not design but **plumbing and proof**: today none of the safety machinery the report relies on exists in code, the eval cannot measure the gates the report proposes, and the Sunday artefacts (health check, watchdog, panic, per-service report) have no plan step. My verdict: agree with the ranked path, but insert one step ("Sunday readiness") before any song runs autonomously, make the FreeShow read-back the thing that scores shadow Sundays automatically, and tighten the acceptance numbers so they are measured by a tool, not read off a checklist.

Findings ranked by severity:

## 2. SLOs, how they are measured, and the alert conditions

The report's metric table is sound; the thresholds are reasonable proposals. My changes: add availability and pass time (the two things that fail silently), state the unit (occurrences, not Sundays), and give every SLO a column in a tool so no human reads a checklist to decide.

**Published SLOs (per service, measured after every rehearsal and service by `voxxa-eval --service-report`):**

| # | SLO | Target | Measured how | Alert |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Spurious presses (`false_advances`) | 0 | exists (`score.rs`) | any → demote the song; incident write-up |
| S2 | Early moves | 0 beyond 1.0 s live; report count beyond 0.3 s | **new** signed-error column `early_300`, `early_1000` (needs `EARLY_MS` logic split from the match window, F3) | any beyond 1.0 s → demote; > 2 beyond 0.3 s in a song → review before next service |
| S3 | Wrong-slide seconds | ≤ 10 s per service; ≤ 3 s per song | `wrong_slide_s` (V3d task 2) | service > 10 s → the service does not count toward promotion |
| S4 | Recall of sung occurrences within 1.5 s | ≥ 0.95 per song for promotion; ≥ 0.95 per service | `recall` + `median_ms`/`p95_ms`, `tight_300` (V3b task 2) | song < 0.90 → "rehearse again" |
| S5 | **Follower availability**: share of sung seconds in which the follower was armed (health green, no watchdog hold, no panic) | ≥ 0.99 | **new**: from `follow`/watchdog/operator lines | < 0.99 or any panic → incident write-up |
| S6 | **Pass time** during singing | median ≤ 1.0 s, p95 ≤ 1.5 s | **new**: per-hop s/pass line | p95 > 1.5 s for 30 s → watchdog hold, amber "slow"; health check red if the canary replay exceeds the rehearsal's time by 1.5× |
| S7 | Audio: dropouts, level in band, hum floor | 0 dropouts; peaks −20..−6 dBFS for ≥ 95% of sung seconds; floor ≥ 30 dB below peaks | **new**: 1 Hz summary of the existing `MicLevel` (`listen.rs:17-22`) | out of band 30 s → amber; sound without words 30 s → watchdog hold (F5) |
| S8 | Read-back agreement (FreeShow) | 100% of moves confirmed by `get_output` within 750 ms | **new**: dispatch `t_sent`, `t_ack` | any disagreement → `position_uncertain` (ADR-025 § 2), hold |
| S9 | Move speed, decision → command | p95 ≤ 100 ms (spec § 2.2) | `t_decision`, `t_sent` | report only |
| S10 | Overrides and hold time | trend down over services; ≥ 2 overrides in a song → demote | **new**: operator lines (hold, resume, panic, F13/F14, accept, dismiss) | hold > 60 s in a service or any panic → review |

**Truth.** Rehearsals: hindsight alignment (report step 5) once V12 exists; tapped truth until then. Services with FreeShow: `get_output` polled at 2 Hz in shadow gives the operator's moves as truth (minus the 524 ms calibrated lag); S2 therefore uses the 1.0 s line live and the 0.3 s line only on rehearsal truth. Services on Presenter: tapped truth as prompt 07 says, or "not scored". (inferred)

**Promotion pass per song** (tool-computed): latest confirmed rehearsal or scored shadow run has S1 = 0, S2 beyond 0.3 s = 0, S4 ≥ 0.95, no `lost`, S6 within target. **Service-level acceptance for running without a volunteer:** ≥ 300 scored occurrences over ≥ 4 services with S1–S3 and S5 met on every one, the last two clean on every song, two volunteers passed the card, one fault drill run. (opinion; a 4–5 song service is roughly 60–75 occurrences, so this is the report's four Sundays in evidence units.)

## 4. Observability

**Per-service log (numbers only).** The session log keeps words for replay (ADR-013, ADR-023 § 6) and stays on the PC. The Sunday artefact is a second, derived file `<church_root>\reports\service-<date>-<slot>.json`, written by `voxxa-eval --service-report <session.jsonl> [--truth …]` the same day, holding only: manifest (version, model SHA, backend, config hash, setlist fingerprint, per-song practice fingerprint and mode); health-check rows (code, value, colour, t); per hop: `t`, s/pass, lag ms, dropped flag; audio at 1 Hz: peak, rms dBFS; per `follow` line: ADR-022 § 6 and ADR-031 § 8 fields (`occ, word, m, A, d, r, gate, why, part, ptr, arm`); per dispatch: `t_decision, t_sent, t_ack`, result code, read-back show/layout/index; watchdog events: kind, value, action; operator events: kind (hold, resume, panic, next, back, accept, dismiss), t; song mode changes: song index, from → to, reason code; the SLO table S1–S10. No `text`, no titles: songs by plan position, slides by index.

**Incident write-up, one paragraph (template `kit/templates/incident.template.md`, ≤ 120 words):** service id · song position and slide indices · what the screen showed versus what was sung, as indices and seconds · what the log says (`why`, gate, watchdog kind) · what the human did and when (hold/panic/manual, `t`) · failure-mode number from § 3 or "new" · fix or ticket id. Stored beside the report; a numbers-only copy may become a GitHub issue (process § 3 privacy rule).

**Overrides and hold time trended:** `voxxa-eval --service-report` appends one row per service to `<church_root>\reports\trend.csv` (date, songs, occurrences, S1–S10, n_hold, hold_s, n_panic, n_next, n_back, n_accept, n_dismiss, songs_demoted). The monthly skill session reads the last eight rows; the kit's prompt 07 states the trend in one sentence. Alert conditions are in § 2.
