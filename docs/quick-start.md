# Voxxa quick-start (Windows)

Voxxa listens to the worship music and changes the slides in your presentation app for
you. It never puts lyrics on the projector; your presentation app does that.

## Install

Download the Windows installer from the
[releases page](https://github.com/nategroks/voxxa/releases) and run it. Open **Voxxa**.

## First start: the Set-up guide (about 10 minutes)

1. **Which microphone?** Pick the input that carries the music, usually the sound
   desk's USB or line input. **Next**.
2. **Find your presentation app.** Open ProPresenter, FreeShow, OpenLP or OpenSong
   first. Pick yours from the list. None found: Voxxa presses the arrow keys instead,
   so keep your presentation app in front during the service. **Next**.
3. **Get the voice model.** Click **Download** (Tiny, 75 MB, once). **Next**.
4. **20-second test.** Load this Sunday's songs (**Choose song files**), click **Start
   the 20-second test**, and play 20 seconds of a song from the list through the
   speakers. "You're ready" means Voxxa followed the song and changed the slide.

**Skip setup** closes the guide; **Advanced → Set-up guide** opens it again.

## Every Sunday: three controls

- **Off / Songs**: choose **Songs** when the music starts, **Off** when you are done.
  (**Songs + Scripture** comes in a later update.)
- **Call for help**: see below.
- **Advanced**: song files, settings and models. **Simple mode** brings you back.

Under the light, Voxxa shows the song and slide it is on: titles only, never lyrics.

## The light

| Colour | What it means |
|---|---|
| Grey | Off. The sentence says what is still missing, or "Choose Songs to start following." |
| Amber | Listening and waiting for a song from the list, or keeping the screen blank on purpose (talking, prayer, a long pause). |
| Green | Following a song: "Following ..., slide 2 of 6." |
| Red | Something needs you. Click **What to do** under the light for the steps. |

The red lights and their steps: [microphone](runbooks/no-audio-device.md),
[presentation app](runbooks/presentation-app-not-reachable.md). The grey "Download the
voice model" light links to [voice model missing](runbooks/model-missing.md).

## Call for help

1. Click **Call for help**. Voxxa shows every file it will save: a report on the app and
   this computer, your settings (secrets and song lists removed), and, if Voxxa recorded
   words recently, the last 2 minutes of words it heard. Untick **Include the last 2
   minutes of words the app heard** to leave that file out.
2. Click **Save and open e-mail**. Voxxa saves one zip file on your Desktop and opens
   an e-mail and the Desktop folder. Nothing is sent by itself.
3. Type the address of the person who helps you with Voxxa, drag the zip file into the
   e-mail, and send it. If the slides stop moving, change them by hand meanwhile.
