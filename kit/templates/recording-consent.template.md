# Recording consent (one form per person)

**This template is not legal advice; have the church's counsel review it.** Change the wording to fit the
church and the law where it meets; keep every section.

Print it, fill it in, sign it, scan it, and file the scan as `<church_root>\consent\<yyyy-mm-dd>-<name>.pdf`
before the first recording. Keep the paper copy where the church keeps other signed forms. The form holds no
song words.

---

## 1. What is recorded

voxxa is a program on the church's sound-booth computer. It listens to the sound desk and moves the song slides.
To test and improve it, the church would like to record (tick what you agree to):

- [ ] **Rehearsals** (the band practising before a service)
- [ ] **Services** (the whole service through the sound desk; see section 4 for talk and prayer)
- [ ] **voxxa's text log**: the words voxxa heard, written to a file on the booth computer and deleted after
      14 days
- [ ] **Audio recordings kept until ____________** (date): the sound of the rehearsals or services ticked above,
      saved as an audio file on the booth computer and deleted by this date (the retention date in section 5).
      Without this box no audio is kept: voxxa only listens, and keeps only its text log if that box is ticked

From (date): ____________  To (date, or "until I withdraw"): ____________

## 2. Why

Only to test how well voxxa follows the songs **on this church's booth computer**, and to let voxxa learn each
song's order and pace from rehearsals. The results kept are numbers (for example, "18 of 19 slides on time").
Nothing is used for advertising, published, or sold.

## 3. Where it is kept

- On the church's booth computer only, in a private folder, and in the church's encrypted backup drive kept at
  the church.
- **Never uploaded, e-mailed, put in a cloud folder, or sent to the voxxa developers or anyone else.**
- Only numbers that hold no voice, no name and no words may leave the booth computer.

## 4. Talk and prayer

Before a recording is kept as a test file, every stretch of speaking (prayer, sermon, announcements, readings,
talk between songs) is replaced with silence. Only the start and end times of those stretches are kept.

## 5. How long

The recording is deleted by (retention date): ____________. A later date needs your agreement in writing. Older
weekly backup copies are gone within 8 weeks after that date.

## 6. You can withdraw at any time

Tell the person below, in writing or in person. Within **7 days** the church deletes every recording with your
voice, every test file made from them and their backup copies. You do not have to give a reason. Numbers already
worked out that hold no voice, no name and no words may stay.

Contact for questions or withdrawal: ______________________________  (name and phone or e-mail)

## 7. Children and young people

If the person recorded is under 18 (or under the age of consent where the church is), a parent or guardian
signs below as well.
