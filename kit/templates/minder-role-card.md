Church: ______________  Sunday: __________  Minder: ______________

**You don't run the slides. You make sure voxxa is allowed to.**

1. **Before the service:** run the health check and read the colour of its top line.
   Green or amber: go. Red: tell the tech lead, and run the slides yourself.
2. **Know three keys:** **HOLD** (F15) stops voxxa moving slides; press it again to carry on.
   **Back one** (F14) goes back one slide. **STOP FOLLOWING** (F20) stops voxxa moving slides for the rest
   of the service; you move them, and voxxa still listens.
3. **If you are unsure, press HOLD.** Nothing moves until you press it again. Still unsure? Press STOP
   FOLLOWING and run the slides yourself. Nobody will mind.
4. **Who to tell:** __________________ (tech lead, phone ______________). After the service, one line: what
   happened, which song (its number in the service), roughly when.

**The badge on each song**

| Badge | What you do |
|---|---|
| voxxa moves it | nothing; watch |
| voxxa suggests | move the slide when the band moves on |
| You move it | run the slides as before |

If you move a slide yourself, voxxa follows on from the slide you chose.
Keep Presenter in front: use the pedal or Stream Deck, and if you click voxxa, click back into Presenter.

**Rule of the room:** nothing on the screen beats the wrong thing on the screen.
